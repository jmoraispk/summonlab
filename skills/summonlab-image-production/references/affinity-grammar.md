# Affinity grammar

Affinity must change at least two meaningful dimensions among material, silhouette, motion,
anatomy, equipment, attack telegraph, environmental wake, and counterplay. Color alone never
qualifies.

Affinity may modify the material and presentation of a declared telegraph or weak point. It cannot
replace the battlefield duty or add a gameplay attack unless the target explicitly declares that
change.

## Normal

Use established species materials and restrained neutral effects. Reject unexplained elemental
glow, magical anatomy, or a hidden affinity.

## Wood

Use bark grain, roots, vines, resin, wooden articulation, or living growth with plausible load
bearing. Reject a brown recolor, decorative antlers, and foliage pasted over unchanged anatomy.

## Fire

Use heat-blackened surfaces, ember seams, combustion, smoke, and localized heat distortion. Reject
a uniform orange body glow or effects that destroy surface and anatomy detail.

## Metal

Use forged anatomy, articulated plates, metallic marrow, controlled fragments, or magnetic force.
For armored species, distinguish the affinity from ordinary equipment. Reject simple armor upgrades.

## Water

Use flow, pressure, mist, wet reflection, and readable fluid transitions with a stable combat
structure. Reject shapeless blue blobs and generic blue glow.

## Wind

Use directional fur or cloth, vapor channels, debris flow, pressure distortion, and a clearly
legible force direction. Reject pale recolors, transparency alone, and directionless particles.

## Construction modes

- Undead: transform binding, armor behavior, weapon, weak point, and command wake.
- Substance-bodied: construct the anatomy from the affinity material around a readable core.
- Organic beast: integrate affinity into fur, hide, breath, claws, motion, and local anatomy.
- Humanoid: combine physiology, clothing, equipment, tool, and combat telegraph without erasing the
  manifest-owned individual.
- Draconic or reptilian: integrate affinity into scales, throat or gaze organs, locomotion, and
  signature attacks without changing the limb plan.
