# Species grammar

Read the section matching the target species. Preserve these cues across every affinity.

## Family profile (v3)

The current `family-affinity-target-v1` plus its hash-bound `family-packet-v1` are the authoritative
species and form grammar. Preserve its family/form identity invariants, tier, anatomy, battlefield
duty, telegraph, counterplay, construction dimensions, and forbidden treatments exactly. Do not
substitute a similarly named legacy species section below. The sections below remain guidance only
for legacy v1/v2 targets.

## Death Knight

- Tall, disciplined armored corpse and formation commander.
- Skull or death mask, small command pennant, deliberate sword guard, and command seal beneath the
  gorget.
- Believable armor load paths and restrained military precision.
- Reject giant implausible swords, random horns, generic living knights, and affinity effects that
  hide the command cues.

## Bound Vanguard Elemental

- One fixed experimental two-arm/two-leg vanguard chassis: compact exposed core, low center of
  gravity, wedge forearms, grounded advance, and one readable impact telegraph.
- Readable combat body, locomotion, concentrated core, and exposed binding structure.
- Affinity substance bears load and forms the anatomy; it is not an external particle costume.
- Normal uses rough tower stone, dust, and pale neutral arcane binding.
- Reject generic humanoid recolors and substance effects that erase the action silhouette.

## Gray Wolf

- Canine head, shoulders, spine, tail, four legs, and fast flanking posture.
- Affinity integrates through coat, breath, claws, locomotion, and environmental wake.
- Reject humanoid armor, antlers, wings, oversized heads, and draconic anatomy.

## Cave Bear

- Ursine head and muzzle, heavy shoulders, powerful forelimbs, short tail, and grounded four-legged
  bruiser mass.
- Affinity integrates without making the bear a large wolf or humanoid golem.

## Goblin

- Adult goblinoid anatomy with individual face, ears, posture, body, clothing, duty, and equipment.
- Preserve every manifest-owned attribute; affinity remains independent of duty and build.
- Reject repeated default faces, identical uniforms, role-affinity stereotypes, human glamour,
  sexualization, and ogre proportions.

## Tunnel Boar

- Low charging body, thick neck, load-bearing shoulders, cloven feet, tusks, and head-down line
  breaker silhouette.
- Reject generic horned beasts and affinity effects that obscure the charge line.

## Cave Drake

- Wingless low quadrupedal dragon, digging shoulders and foreclaws, battering skull, tail balance,
  and readable throat vulnerability.
- Reject wings, wyvern anatomy, serpentine anatomy, and implausible joints.

## Tower Basilisk

- Low heavy reptile, manifest-declared limb plan, rigid group of forward-facing mineral gaze organs,
  narrow attack lane, and vulnerable blind flank. The affinity experiment currently declares six
  grounded legs; the ambiguous family plate is not leg-count evidence.
- Reject snakes, amorphous eye masses, wrong limb counts, and affinity effects that hide the gaze
  direction.

## Expansion goblinoids

- Goblin Skirmisher, Archer, Hexweaver, and Chieftain are adult goblins with exactly two arms and two
  legs; preserve their knife, shortbow, hex-focus, or command-and-cleaver duty respectively.
- Vary face structure, age, build, clothing layers, footwear, headwear, and material wear without
  turning a role into another role. Reject cloned faces, cloned uniforms, childlike bodies, human
  glamour, and ogre mass.

## Dire Wolf

- Exactly four canine legs, a dense pursuit frame, recognizable wolf head, grounded paws, and a
  coherent tail. It is larger and more relentless than the Gray Wolf without becoming a bear or
  dragon.

## Expansion elementals

- Ember Wisp is a small airborne coal/mineral core with asymmetric flame and a directional spark
  wake; reject a humanoid or featureless orb.
- Stone Breaker is a planted quarry-stone biped with wedge forearms, low center of gravity, and
  readable binding seams.
- Storm Stalker is an exactly four-legged thunderhead with coherent skeletal current and aligned
  forward prongs; reject a natural wolf recolor.
- Frost Colossus is a towering glacier biped with one dominant sweeping arm and a visible stone
  heart; reject an ordinary organic Frost Giant.

## Constructs

- Clay Golem, Stone Golem, Iron Sentinel, and Crystal Guardian remain two-arm/two-leg manufactured
  bodies organized around credible joints and one controlling core.
- Preserve hand-shaped clay compression, block-stone guard slabs, disciplined forged plating, or
  faceted refractive planes respectively. Affinity changes construction rather than coating a living
  humanoid.

## Expansion undead

- Grave Sentinel is a grounded shield-first corpse in collapsed burial armor with visible binding
  nails.
- Tower Wraith has one shrouded humanoid upper body, long connected arms, a solid mask, no feet, and
  a tapering spectral body.
- Bone Mage is one coherent humanoid skeleton whose rib-like focus supports a readable trajectory;
  reject a staff-wielding living mage or disconnected bone cloud.

## Expansion draconic creatures

- Venom Wyvern has exactly two hind legs and two wings, with no separate forelegs, plus a hooked tail
  stinger.
- Armored Elder Drake is a wingless four-legged drake with overlapping mineral plates, furnace
  throat, and exposed plate gaps.
- Giant Tower Dragon has exactly four legs plus two wings and a complete apex-scale head, neck,
  torso, tail, and architectural wing silhouette.

## Dungeon predators

- Hungry Mimic is one broken chest-bodied ambush creature with connected hinges, weight-bearing
  jaws, short tongue, and support limbs.
- Slime Colony is one connected low membrane containing several separately readable dense cores.
- Burrowing Worm is one continuous limbless segmented body with a circular grinding mouth, armored
  front rings, and a softer rear.

## Large threats

- Ogoblin Brute is a squat boss-scale goblinoid bruiser; Cave Troll is a stooped gray cavern giant
  with disproportionately long arms; Hill Giant is upright with a practical stone maul; Frost Giant
  is an organic cold-adapted giant in fur and ancient iron.
- Every giant has exactly two arms and two legs. Reject cloned bare-chested loincloth silhouettes and
  do not turn the Frost Giant into an elemental or golem.

## Arcane hostiles

- Hex Adept, Ward Mage, Tower Summoner, and Tower Archmage are distinct adult humans with exactly two
  arms and two legs. Preserve their curse tags, geometric forearm ward, exactly two grounded pylons,
  or exactly three distinct casting anchors respectively.
- Faces, skin tone, hair, gender presentation, build, and clothing are independent dimensions. Use
  practical non-sexualized fantasy clothing and reject repeated faces, repeated full outfits,
  age-incoherent hair, and generic long robes.

## Tower arachnids

- Tunnel Spider, Web Caster, Armored Brood Guard, and Giant Broodmother each have exactly eight
  connected locomotor legs—four per side—with all eight roots and terminal feet individually
  countable. Pedipalps and chelicerae are not legs.
- Preserve the sprinting low body, raised silk-casting abdomen, plated chokepoint guard, or enormous
  suspended brood abdomen respectively. Reject six or ten legs, fused roots, hidden rear legs,
  duplicate spiders, and insect wings.
