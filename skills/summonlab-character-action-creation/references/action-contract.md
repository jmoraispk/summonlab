# Character action brief

Write one approved brief before animation production:

```text
Character / action:
Gameplay intent:
Motion sentence:
Starting pose / prerequisite state:
1. Beat one:
2. Beat two:
3. Beat three:
4. Beat four:
5. Beat five:
Acting limb / delivery:
Target direction / contact beat:
Contacts / center of mass:
Equipment / identity constraints:
Loop seam or terminal state:
Forbidden alternatives:
```

## Acceptance gates

- One readable action verb and one acting limb or delivery mechanism remain constant across all five
  beats.
- Name the exact pose and runtime state that precede beat one, including any guard, footing, charge,
  target, or equipment prerequisite the action depends on.
- Beats have causal order: preparation, delivery or contact, consequence, and ending cannot be
  rearranged or duplicated as progress.
- Declare the target direction and the one beat where contact, release, or maximum extension occurs.
- Record every planted foot, paw, hand, body support, release, and center-of-mass transfer that
  affects the action.
- Lock identity-defining anatomy, costume, equipment design, handedness, and any item that must remain
  attached or follow a continuous release path.
- A terminal action moves monotonically toward its terminal state; it does not regain unresolved
  recovery energy.
- A loop explains its recovery-to-start seam, including contacts and equipment placement.
- List incompatible actions, limbs, contacts, effects, or endings under `Forbidden alternatives`.
