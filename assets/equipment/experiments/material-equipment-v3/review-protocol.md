# Equipment v3 review protocol

## Checkpoint 1

Review all 11 class progression sheets at full resolution plus 128px and 256px thumbnails. Decide accept, revise, or defer for the four new directions. No root assignment exists until all four are accepted.

## Checkpoint 2

Review the 11 deterministic class audits first, then 28 material audits, then each of 77 dedicated roots. Rank clarity wins over material uniformity. A full Armor set additionally must be empty, self-supporting, and show exactly Head, Torso, Legs, and Boots before it can receive an unlock receipt.

## Checkpoint 3

Review 21 audits in strict order: Full Set, Head, Torso, Legs, Boots. Each piece must preserve the exact accepted source's material, silhouette language, palette, trim, fastenings, wear, ornament, and slot boundary.

Checkpoint receipts are hash-bound and owner-authored. Review evidence does not promote a candidate to approved or runtime state.
