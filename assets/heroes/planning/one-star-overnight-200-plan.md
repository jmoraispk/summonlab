## Outcome

Produce **200 completely new 1★ adult heroes**, excluding every identity already reviewed in waves
001 and 002. Each review candidate is a pair:

1. one full-resolution cinematic semi-realistic painted summon portrait;
2. one identity-matched combat chibi derived from that exact portrait.

The target is therefore 200 reviewable heroes and 400 base images, plus a bounded correction
allowance. Existing Keeps appear only in a separate reference shelf and do not count toward 200.
Every output remains candidate-only until explicit human selection and later runtime preparation.

## Fixed uniformity

Every target uses the same production grammar:

- exactly one weaponless adult, full body, both hands and both feet visible;
- calm neutral expression and grounded three-quarter standing pose;
- warm pale parchment studio wash, soft grounding shadow, and no environmental storytelling;
- cinematic semi-realistic **painted drawing**, with visible brush planes and no photographic pores,
  lens treatment, plastic 3D, or cel-anime finish;
- ordinary but intentionally designed 1★ mortal clothing: no magic, aura, affinity effect, floating,
  crown, elite armor, frame, printed star, number, UI, or text;
- one memorable identity anchor, but no requirement that the anchor be a tattoo or jewelry;
- fixed summon review canvas of 1024 × 1536 and fixed chibi review canvas of 1024 × 1536;
- original generated bytes preserved separately from normalized review derivatives;
- chibis remain approximately 2.75–3.1 heads tall. A tall hero is expressed through narrowness,
  posture, hair, and relative limb language—not by shrinking the head until the result stops reading
  as chibi;
- chibi cuteness comes from the head, face readability, and shape simplification. It must not come
  from generic shoulder, torso, upper-arm, forearm, hand, or body-fat inflation;
- the summon portrait is the sole anatomy, face, hair, wardrobe, palette, accessory, and marking
  authority for its chibi.

Dyed blue, teal, violet, pink, or multicolor hair is personal fashion at 1★ and does not imply an
affinity. No affinity-colored glow or gem is permitted.

## Character kernel timing

Generate a compact personality-and-background kernel **before** the portrait, but do not write a
finished biography for all 200 candidates.

The project canon gives new summons a starting temperament plus fragments of language, custom,
gesture, taste, and cultural intuition, but no autobiographical memory of where those fragments came
from. The pre-art kernel therefore contains:

- one high-salience temperament trait, one counter-trait, and one useful tension or contradiction;
- social presentation such as guarded, open, formal, blunt, playful, attentive, restless, or calm;
- grooming and maintenance attitude;
- color confidence and ornament tolerance;
- two cultural fragments expressed through material, pattern, custom, gesture, food, or taste;
- one noncanonical “life echo” such as textile work, field labor, study, medicine, trade, travel,
  performance, administration, ritual practice, construction, or domestic care;
- one short art-facing posture or gesture cue;
- one sentence describing what must **not** be inferred from the body, face, gender, or phenotype.

The kernel is randomized independently from physiology, phenotype, aptitude, rarity, morality, and
combat role. A huge bodybuilder may be quiet and bookish; a slight person may be confrontational; a
carefully dressed scholar may be disorganized; a visibly repaired outfit may belong to someone vain
about craft. These contradictions are intentional, not errors.

Every kernel must pass a personality-strength gate: after hiding the name and body description, its
dominant trait, counter-trait, social edge, and visual consequence must still differ meaningfully
from the preceding candidates. “Nice, brave, calm” or another generic agreeable cluster is a retry.
Strong personality does not mean exaggerated facial acting; the owner has already rejected smiles,
grins, and theatrical expressions as a substitute for character.

Only drawable consequences enter the image prompt: posture, gaze, grooming, maintenance, garment
order or repair, pattern density, color confidence, ornament restraint, and one personal object.
Abstract adjectives do not directly instruct facial anatomy, intelligence, ethnicity, beauty, or
muscle.

After a pair receives Keep, expand the kernel into voice, values, drives, fears, preferences, and
other authored character material. Bind the accepted five-category aptitude packet separately and
only through the progression authority; do not revive the legacy four-score aptitude model. The
hero's actual biography, relationships, habits, and remembered history then grow through play after
summoning.

collapse into stereotypes.

### Skin and facial variety

Use six broad skin-value bands with totals of 24, 30, 34, 38, 38, and 36 from lightest to deepest.

shuffled independently; no phenotype receives a costume stereotype.

### Coolness anchor deck

Every hero draws one primary recognition strategy, with exactly 25 heroes in each family:

1. hairstyle construction or hair mass;
2. garment silhouette or hem geometry;
3. unusual but bounded color blocking;
4. textile pattern, quilting, embroidery, or stitching;
5. distinctive body construction or habitual posture;
6. repairs, patina, customization, or keepsake;
7. jewelry, piercing, clasp, buckle, or hair hardware;
8. tattoo, scar, freckle field, birthmark, or durable body marking.

Secondary details may support the primary anchor, but they cannot make every hero equally ornate.
This deck creates deliberate peaks of personality while preserving the shared 1★ power ceiling.

`roster-plan.json` contains 200 active manifests plus 20 reserve manifests. A fixed planning seed

paths, symlinked destinations, duplicate IDs, quota drift, or cross-worker ownership are hard
preflight failures. Each target also owns its compact character kernel; later narrative expansion is
kept out of the image-production critical path.

## Overnight orchestration

### Stage 1 — preflight and calibration

- Validate all quota totals, names, paths, references, prompt bindings, and output ownership.
- Generate eight calibration pairs: four men and four women spanning slight, soft, average,
  plus-size, athletic, and muscular bodies; include natural and dyed hair.
- An inspecting agent checks rendering style, full-body composition, 1★ power ceiling, face/body
  individuality, and chibi translation before bulk dispatch.
- A failed calibration pauses only the affected prompt family. It does not rewrite the already
  accepted rendering reference or shared runtime systems.

### Stage 2 — parallel pair production

- Dispatch 12 producer agents plus one coordinator/auditor, subject to actual available capacity.
- Each producer receives a disjoint hero-ID list and may write only its assigned target, candidate,
  preview, and result paths.
- Work is pipelined per hero: generate and inspect the summon first, then derive and inspect its
  chibi. A hero is not published to the morning page until both sides exist and pass basic gates.
- Produce ten review waves of 20 complete pairs. Validate, commit, and push each completed wave so a
  late failure cannot erase the night.
- The coordinator alone writes the aggregate report and review index after verifying all per-hero
  results and hashes.

### Stage 3 — bounded correction and reserves

The base budget is 400 calls. Allow at most 60 additional correction calls across the entire run.
Each image receives at most one source-bound correction for a hard failure. Corrections may fix
composition, extra limbs, photographic drift, chibi head ratio, or clear anatomy mismatch; they may
not opportunistically redesign a valid person.

If a target still fails, quarantine it with its evidence and activate the next unused reserve
manifest. Never insert a broken pair, quietly alter quota truth, or copy another hero to hit 200.
The morning report states the number of complete pairs, quarantined attempts, reserves used,
provider failures, and remaining shortfall. The target is 200; if external generation capacity is
lower overnight, the page exposes only complete pairs rather than pretending incomplete work is
finished.

## Per-image quality gates

### Summon gate

- exactly one adult and no duplicated anatomy;
- full head, both hands, and both feet present with safe margin;
- unmistakably painted at first glance and within the accepted realism ceiling;
- manifest body, height, hair silhouette, clothing family, palette, and detail density are legible;
- no weapon, magic, frame, star, text, affinity cue, smile, or exaggerated acting;
- not a near-duplicate of an existing reviewed hero or another overnight candidate.

### Chibi gate

- clearly the same face, age cues, skin, hair construction, wardrobe, palette, accessories, and marks;
- 2.75–3.1-head chibi ratio, including tall and gangly heroes;
- shoulder width, torso width, arm and forearm girth, wrist scale, mass, softness, and muscle remain
  faithful to the summon;
- hands are simplified but do not become disproportionately huge unless that is an explicit source
  trait;
- clean silhouette, full extremities, grounded ready-idle pose, no weapon or supernatural effect;
- background/alpha failures are recorded honestly. Candidate checker backgrounds may remain until
  selection, but no file is mislabeled as transparent.

## Morning review experience

The page always shows summon and chibi beside one another. There is no isolated-view toggle.

- Load 20 pairs per page and lazy-load compressed review derivatives so 400 full-resolution images
  do not overwhelm the browser; clicking either side opens the preserved source.
- Keep the reference shelf separate from the 200 new candidates.
- Provide `Keep`, `Pass`, `Revise summon`, `Revise chibi`, `Revise both`, and `Undecided` controls,
  plus a short optional note.
- Persist locally after every edit and export one JSON containing decisions and notes.
- Show completion counts and filters. Keep body/hair manifest labels hidden by default for visual
  review, with an optional “reveal brief” control for diagnosing a mismatch.
- Include filters for decision state and wave, not a mode that separates the pair.
- Serve persistently on `0.0.0.0`; every collaborator URL uses `10.110.24.218`.
