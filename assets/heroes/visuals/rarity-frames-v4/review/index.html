Frozen production rules

These accepted constraints are checksum-bound with the visual sources so later work does not reinterpret them.

One integrated object

Tray and stars share the frame’s rendered bevel, light, shadow, and occlusion. No independent star overlays.

Stable hero opening

Within each 1★–7★ family, widest opening divided by narrowest stays at or below 1.20.

Delayed affinity

Ranks 1–2 are byte-identical neutral frames. Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind begin at rank 3.

Awakening gate

Dragon mouths stay sealed through 6★. Only 7★ opens both mouths into a readable roar.
