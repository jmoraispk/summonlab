# ADR 0034: First-band content carries cross-floor evidence

Status: accepted on 2026-08-24.

## Context

Floors 02–05 need to establish a coherent first arc before their mechanics are connected to the
runtime. Floor 05 must support an obvious direct assault and a preparation-sensitive cooling route,
but exposing the hidden route as ordinary map knowledge would erase the value of curiosity, memory,
and trust on Floor 04. Asking an agent to invent either route would also violate the rule that agents
choose legal actions but never author world truth or mutate state.

## Decision

- `tower-band-01-v1` is a verified ordered catalog of the five immutable, SHA-bound Floor packages.
  Every package keeps one default objective route, visit/discovery-bounded observations, explicit
  monster duties and environmental policies, an authored extraction boundary, an exact reward
  budget, and a fixed five-anchor story envelope with one bounded optional patch slot.
- Floors 02–04 provide distinct band jobs: formation/protection, injury/retreat pressure, then
  one-person separation and trust. Floor 04 can reveal the canonical Foundry maintenance schematic.
- Floor 05 places `foundry-warden-v1` exactly once. Its exposed direct route is always structurally
  available. Its maintenance affordance requires the explicit `relay-route-memory` capability and
  reveals only the authored maintenance seam, cooling control, and connecting edges.
- Matching discovery ids across Floor 04 and Floor 05 identify related authored facts but do not
  automatically transfer knowledge. `MIS-006` must derive the capability from persisted engine
  evidence and own cooling state, armor changes, and replay.
- The Mission Lab may simulate retained route memory to inspect the content boundary, but it remains
  a read-only content and knowledge projection rather than a run authority.

## Consequences

The full first band can now be reviewed as a coherent arc and validated before adding more engine
state. Players always retain a costly but straightforward Floor 05 route, while exploration and
trusted evidence can later unlock a mechanically different route. The temporary lab toggle is
honest about what has and has not been implemented: content exists now; causal cross-floor memory
and cooling execution do not exist until `MIS-006`.
