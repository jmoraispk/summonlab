# 0046 — First-band archive truth is extraction-bound

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-25

## Decision

The Floor 05 relight core and archive fragment become canonical only when a successful Living
Foundry party crosses the authored extraction boundary. The archive then moves through a one-way
`sealed → recovered → revealed` lifecycle.

The record itself is fixed: **“Hearth 01 was not the first.”** The player selects one living
extraction survivor to read it. That hero's personality, values, drive, social style, and habit shape
the deterministic delivery, but cannot alter the fact. Only the reader and present witnesses gain
explicit knowledge.

## Consequences

- Warden defeat without extraction cannot grant the relight core or archive fragment.
- The recovery receipt binds its source event, mission attempt, content, reward IDs, survivors, and
  completed story history.
- New summons remain clean slates because knowledge is an explicit holder list, not global lore.
- StoryDirector may affect catalogued expression elsewhere but cannot replace the finale conflict,
  rewards, archive message, or extraction requirement.
- Extraction and reveal must both survive strict replay and database reload.
