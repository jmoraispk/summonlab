# Reversal Cut

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Canonical brief

- **Identity:** `martial:blade:reversal-cut`, Martial → Blade
- **Rarity and type:** A, Active
- **Equipment:** Sword
- **Requires:** Mirrored Edge Master, Riposte Master, and the deed of parrying a named foe's
  signature attack
- **Effect:** After a successful parry, convert the intercepted attack's full force into an
  unblockable returning cut against a chosen target.
- **Visual lineage:** append to `blade.guard-flow` after immediate parent
  `martial:blade:mirrored-edge`
- **Icon subject:** a clearly visible sword snapping from a parry into a tight reverse diagonal
  cut
- **Supporting cue:** one opposing blade and a compact hooked return trail communicating
  redirected force
- **Palette:** tempered steel, charcoal black, cold white-blue motion, and restrained amber impact
  sparks
- **Exclusions:** no static crossed swords, mirror imagery, effect-only slash marks, characters,
  scenery, text, logos, or extra rarity symbols

This is an owner-approved, net-new skill. It has no legacy-compendium source row and must use
explicit net-new provenance rather than a fabricated migration record.
