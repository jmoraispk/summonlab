# Overgeared equipment catalog

Wiki ID: `research.attribute-systems.overgeared.equipment-catalog`

The supplied catalog sampled roughly fifty verified named items and recorded the fan wiki's
larger category counts. Automated access was incomplete, so counts are research snapshots rather
than an authoritative live inventory.

## Structural observations

- The catalog distinguishes weapons, many defensive body slots, accessories, consumables,
  support items, and a large production-material layer.
- Cross-cutting classifications—sets, growth items, ego items, unique-only items, and
  collections—carry progression independently from slot and rarity.
- The documented weapon distribution is heavily sword-skewed because the story follows a
  swordsman/blacksmith. It should not define Summonlab's generation weights.
- The recorded rarity pyramid is inverted, with far more named legendary/myth items than mundane
  ones. This measures narrative attention, not the population of a healthy game economy.
- A deep crafting economy has a substantial content cost: the snapshot records 142 production
  materials beside 322 equipment entries.

## Summonlab use

Use the catalog as a taxonomy and authoring-surface checklist. Do not copy its category
frequencies, rarity distribution, or story-character equipment power. Summonlab should deliberately
choose its slot count, weapon-family coverage, material-to-recipe ratio, and rarity pyramid, then
validate those distributions from generated content.

See [equipment mechanics](equipment-mechanics.md) and [item properties](item-properties.md).
