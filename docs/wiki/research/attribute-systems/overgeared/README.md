# Overgeared attribute and equipment research

Wiki ID: `research.attribute-systems.overgeared`

Research status: **reference, not Summonlab authority**. This section records the useful system
shapes visible in *Overgeared* / *Satisfy*. Its fan-wiki figures are incomplete and occasionally
contradictory, so they are evidence for design comparison rather than source code constants.

## Attribute-system summary

*Overgeared* exposes four freely allocatable primary stats—Strength, Stamina, Agility, and
Intelligence—and keeps Dexterity among special stats that cannot receive ordinary free points.
Every 100 character levels, a stat awakening changes one or more per-point conversion rates without
changing the basic equations.

| Overgeared | Main outcomes | Closest Summonlab concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Physical attack, Health, carry weight | **Strength (`STR`)** |
| Agility | Attack frequency, movement, equipment/mastery-dependent effects | **Agility (`AGI`)** |
| Stamina | Health and defense; distinct from its expendable resource usage | **Vitality (`VIT`)** |
| Intelligence | Magic power, Mana, spell access | **Magic (`MAG`)** |

Summonlab's Strength, Agility, Vitality, and Magic therefore match the four primary roles closely,
but the mechanics are deliberately different. Summonlab uses one complete, terminating ten-row
conversion table; keeps armor, cadence, accuracy, recovery, and most ratios as separate budgets;
and does not add Dexterity as a fifth primary attribute. Full names are normal prose, while compact
character sheets use the fixed order `STR`, `AGI`, `VIT`, `MAG`.

Affinities are only mild, separable associations in Summonlab. The Overgeared comparison does not
make them owners of attributes or conversion rates, and a later rules version may weaken or remove
their relationship.
