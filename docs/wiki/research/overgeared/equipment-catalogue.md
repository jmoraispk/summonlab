# Overgeared — Equipment Catalogue

Wiki ID: `research.overgeared.equipment-catalogue`

Research status: **user-supplied secondary synthesis; not Summonlab canon**.

This component page preserves the supplied catalogue as research evidence. Its interpretations and
design observations are claims from that source, not accepted Summonlab mechanics or implementation
instructions. See the parent
[attribute-system research entry](../attribute-systems.md#overgeared-satisfy) and the companion
[equipment mechanics reference](equipment-mechanics.md).

The inventory side of the reference. Structure, counts, and every named item
verified during research.

**Coverage note, read first.** The Overgeared wiki returns HTTP 402 to automated
fetching, so individual pages could not be opened directly — everything below
came through search indexing of those pages. The category counts are quoted
accurately from the wiki's own index. The named-item entries are the subset whose
properties could be verified; names are not invented to pad the list. Roughly 50
of the ~322 catalogued equipment items appear here. Where a property could not be
confirmed it is marked *(unconfirmed)* rather than guessed.

Companion page: [Equipment mechanics](equipment-mechanics.md) for how these properties work.

The wiki indexes **750 items**, of which **322 are equipment**. The slot
breakdown is the useful artefact — it tells you how a mature item system
distributes its authoring effort.

**Observation, and it is the important one:** the rating distribution is
*inverted*. There are 90 legendary and 86 myth items against 14 rare and 19
normal. A shipped game has the opposite pyramid. This is a catalogue of what a
story chose to describe, not of what a world contains — the mundane gear exists
but is never written down.

Economically, rare items are worth about three times normal, and epic about seven
times normal.

## Part 2 — Verified item entries

Grouped by function. `AP` = attack power.

### Weapons — swords

**Sword of Self-transcendence** — Legendary. Durability 365, AP 356, attack speed
+6%, accuracy +10%, attack and defense rate +10%, +200 flat damage per attack.
Grants *Perfect State of Self-transcendence* (all stats double for two minutes
and all abnormal conditions are resisted, but the wearer cannot be controlled for
the duration). Requires level 160+, 950+ strength, Advanced Sword Mastery lv2+.
Grid's first legendary craft; made on commission for the Steim family.

**Failure** — Legendary greatsword. Requires level 300+, **5,000+ strength**,
Advanced Sword Mastery lv8+. Grid's fifth legendary item and the first he
designed himself.

**Iyarugt** — Legendary (growth) / ego one-handed sword. Crit inflicts bleeding
for 3 seconds with bleed damage proportional to attack power; at 3 combos the
bleed maximises and damage to the target rises 200% for 1 second; at 5 combos the
target's thinking is destroyed for 0.3 seconds, opening the linked skill *Hell
Sword*. As it grows, the bleed extends to 5 seconds and the window becomes 300%
for 2 seconds, and *Sword Demon's Senses* is added (extreme evasion for 5
seconds). Conditions of use: a person the sword chooses. Gains experience from
PvE and PvP at comparable rates. Formerly Grid's, later Peak Sword's.

**Dainsleif (Reproduction)** — Unique greatsword. Notably strong against multiple
opponents. Grants *Golden Flash*.

**Mamon's Greatsword** — early-game weapon whose attack power alone let a very
low-level character hunt level-20 monsters.

**Ideal Longsword** — grants *Quick Movements*, same as the Ideal Dagger.

### Weapons — daggers

**Ideal Dagger** — Unique. Requires level 180+, 450+ agility, Advanced Dagger
Mastery. Grants *Quick Movements* (evasion +30% and agility doubled for 1 minute;
80 mana, 100s cooldown) and *Wind Blast*. Crafted with the NPC blacksmith Khan.

**Dagger of Bewitchment** — requires level 100+, 200+ agility, 30+ strength,
Intermediate Dagger Mastery. High attack power, low durability. Signature
*bewitchment* option puts a target into a non-resistant state for 3 seconds.
Explicitly framed as an assassin's weapon. Made by Euphemina in a blacksmith
competition against Grid.

### Weapons — bows

**Thunder Bow** — Unique. Durability 490, AP 370~601 (a range). Arrows cannot be
loaded; 100 mana consumed per attack; grants *Penetrating Flash*; +10% firing
speed. Requires level 300+, 2,000+ agility, Advanced Bow Mastery lv5+. Contains a
Thunder Stone sometimes considered more valuable than the bow.

**Spiral Quick Fire Bow** — each arrow fired raises firing speed by 1%, capped at
+50%. Arrow trajectory is difficult to control. Hitting the intended target
grants bonus Bow Mastery experience. Requires level 300+, Advanced Bow Mastery
lv2+.

**Quick Fire Bow (production method)** — pattern producing Rare~Unique results.
Same requirements as above.

**Angel Bow** — pattern producing Rare~Unique results. Requires level 300+.

**Red Phoenix Bow** — Grid's first myth-rated item; crafting it upgraded his
blacksmithing technique.

### Weapons — spears, axes, hammers

**Gale Spear** — Unique. Requires level 240+, 750+ strength, 400+ agility,
Advanced Spear Mastery lv2+. Very light, framed as both advantage and drawback.
Made for Pon.

**Daoist Woodcutting Axe** — Legendary. Durability 1,000. Emits strong flames each
time durability is lost. Cuts and influences white phosphorus trees specifically.
Conditions of use: Daoist. AP and weight figures ambiguous in the source
*(unconfirmed)*.

**Mjolnir – Human World Version** — one-handed hammer, adamantium. Requires level
350+, 3,000+ strength, **3,500+ dexterity**; weight 4,900. Targets struck are
rigid for 0.3 seconds; hits from other Mjolnirs cause 0.1s stiffness, and
alternating Mjolnirs can chain stiffness indefinitely. Enhanced version reaches
100% accuracy and carries *Magic Detection (Enhanced)* with the accuracy bonus
raised to 50%. The dexterity requirement is explicitly a consequence of designing
for Grid and the God Hands rather than a general gate. Grid made four of these.

**God Hands** — Grid's signature crafted weapons; later versions are ego-type and
myth-rated. Associated with Sword Mastery.

**Blacksmith's Hammer / Smith's Old Axe / Smith's Pickaxe** — early production
tools.

**Unknown Master Blacksmith's Hammer** — raises the chance of producing a
higher-rated item. Occupies a weapon slot, contributes nothing to combat.

### Ammunition

**Special Jaffa Arrow** — Epic. Ignores part or all of the target's defense.
Usable as a spear as well as an arrow.

### Armour — the Holy Light Set

**Holy Light Armor** — Legendary (raised +1 tier by a craft effect), adamantium,
originally name-locked to a specific person. High chance to resist dark magic;
healing effectiveness +300%; increased physical attack resistance. Made by Pagma
for the fifth pope. Later dyed with Firestone Dye; Grid fitted seven Pavranium
blades to it as a tail.

**Holy Light Gloves** — increased attack speed and accuracy; low chance to
activate *5 Joint Attacks*.

**Holy Light Crown** — raises intelligence and dignity.

**Set bonus (3 pieces)** — Defense +500, Health +6,000.

### Armour — other

**Mengel's Plate Armor** — early-game armour granting Strength +10; negated damage
from low-level monsters at low character level.

**Three Layers** — armour set with defense superior to the Holy Light Set, though
the Holy Light Set's adamantium gave it better options and durability.

**Horse Knight's Helmet · Troll Lord's Vest · Crying Ogre's Gauntlets · Golden
Crown's Leggings** — monster-derived pieces Grid set out to reproduce as
replacement armour. Individual options *(unconfirmed)*.

**Lantier's Cloak** — reproduced through Item Creation.

**Alex's Quick Gloves** — roughly halve the basic attack interval. At 2,876
agility this takes a 0.8s interval to 0.4s; at 3,000 agility, 0.7s to 0.35s.

**Perfect Divine Shield** — Legendary. Weakens dark-aligned spells and grants
light-based abilities.

**Mass Production Grid Set** — one-handed swords, spears, bows, shields, helmet,
armor, gloves and shoes, later extended with a cloak and underwear. Strong
performance and a strong set effect relative to other gear of the same level.
Originally made for Reidan's soldiers, later released to players to attract them
to the Overgeared Kingdom.

### Consumables

**Intermediate Agility Potion / Intermediate Strength Potion** — raise the stat
for 5 minutes.

**Sweet Candy** — raises all stats by a fixed amount.

**Ultimate Health Potion** — restores health; 30-second cooldown.

**Advanced Mana Potion** — restores mana over time.

**Agility and Intelligence Elixirs** — permanent stat increases, purchased with
challenger points on Fog Island.

**Weapons Mastery and Magic Mastery skill books** — grant the generalist mastery
skills.

## Part 3 — What the catalogue shows

**Slot count is high and deliberately granular.** Thirteen defensive slots
including underwear, nine accessory slots. Each is a place to hang one small
effect. Summonlab does not need thirteen, but the principle — many small slots
beat few large ones for build expression — is worth weighing against UI cost.

**Weapon type distribution follows narrative, not design.** 90 swords to 2 flutes.
A procedural generator needs a deliberate distribution; this one is an artefact
of what the story spent time on.

**The rating pyramid is inverted, and that is a warning.** 90 legendary and 86
myth against 14 rare. In a real economy the base of the pyramid carries the
weight. For a gacha-adjacent system where rarity must mean something, the
distribution has to be designed explicitly — procedural generation will happily
produce whatever shape the roll tables describe, including a useless one.

**Materials are ~44% of equipment count.** A crafting economy with real depth
costs roughly one material for every two items. Relevant when pricing the Forging
and Alchemy families.

**Cross-cutting classifications carry the interesting mechanics.** Set items (46),
growth items (25), ego items (16). These are not slots or ratings — they are
orthogonal properties that any item can carry, and they are where retention
mechanics live.
