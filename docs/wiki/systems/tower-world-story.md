# Tower, World, and Story

Wiki ID: `system.tower-world-story`

This system owns destinations, tower structure, encounters as content, expeditions, world facts,
and campaign meaning. Combat resolution and permanent hero progression remain owned elsewhere.

## Living Village

Only a static mechanical shell is active:

- immutable Floors 1–10;
- sequential unlock and cleared-floor replay;
- approximately three scaled encounter patterns;
- one party snapshot per attempt;
- monster and reward references through stable IDs.

There is no campaign story, randomization, navigation, discovery, lore delivery, portal expedition,
neighbor, or world simulation in this milestone. Those exclusions let the floor/content contract be
tested before narrative breadth depends on it.

See [Living Village](../milestones/living-village.md) and the
[monster bestiary](../creatures/monster-races/README.md).

## Target two-mode structure

The complete design separates open-ended **Expeditions** from five-floor **Tower Challenges**.
Expeditions allow voluntary return and species-targeted practice. Tower Challenges require five
consecutive, unusually difficult floor battles, while Tower Exploration allows independent parties
to practice a selected floor concurrently without clearing the challenge sequence.

Each authored floor has one primary monster family. A family supplies five forms whose visual tier
and combat archetype advance together from T1 through T5; the T5 Sovereign is the floor's capstone
boss. Thirty consolidated families can therefore cover thirty primary-family floors. How those
floors are ordered into towers, regions, and release bands is reserved for the dedicated combat and
floor-progression specification.

Party requirements, sequential challenge completion, concurrent exploration ownership, and
species-practice unlocks are defined in
[Summoning, Progression, and Combat](summoning-progression-combat.md#target-combat-modes-and-party-requirements).
