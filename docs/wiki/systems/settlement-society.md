# Settlement and Society

Wiki ID: `system.settlement-society`

This system eventually owns the physical town, facilities, capacity and expansion together with
hero agency, relationships, institutions, motivation, and social life. Agency belongs here rather
than being bundled into numerical hero growth.

## Living Village

The settlement is intentionally static. It provides three fixed affordances:

- the Summoning Gate acquires heroes;
- the Training Court reserves heroes for direct player-assigned training;
- the Tower Threshold deploys one party.

There are no autonomous choices, personalities, moods, relationships, requests, schedules,
staffing, construction, upgrades, food, or society simulation. Heroes expose only `idle`,
`training`, or `deployed` availability. This is a deliberate milestone boundary, not a rejection of
the later society-centered game.
