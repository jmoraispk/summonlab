# Fire

Wiki ID: `affinity.fire`

**Definition:** Expansion, aggression, and consumption.

**Proposed status:** **Burn** — damage over time; intensity stacks rather than duration.

**Matching attribute:** Might. The candidate nudge is capped at 10% of Might's contribution; it is
not a direct Might point on level-up and is not yet applied by production combat.

**Material language:** Vermilion embers, localized heat distortion, material glow, smoke, ash, and
cooling residue.

See the canonical [Affinities](../progression/affinities.md) rules.
