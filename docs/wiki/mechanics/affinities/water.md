# Water

Wiki ID: `affinity.water`

**Definition:** Flow, depth, and patience.

**Proposed status:** **Chill** — reduces attack speed and mana regeneration; escalates toward
torpor.

**Mildly associated attribute:** Magic. The candidate nudge is capped at 10% of Magic's
contribution; it is not a direct Magic point on level-up, is not yet applied by production combat,
and may be separated in a later rules version.

**Material language:** Deep-blue condensation, flowing water, and residue.

See the canonical [Affinities](../progression/affinities.md) rules.
