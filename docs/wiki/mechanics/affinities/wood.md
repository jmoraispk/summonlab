# Wood affinity

Wiki ID: `affinity.wood`

Design status: **draft**

## Player-facing rule

Wood affinity represents living growth, roots, fibers, adaptation, and patient resilience—not merely
lumber as a material. Matching actions may gain a bounded chance to apply **Slow** through binding,
entanglement, or obstructive growth.

## Mechanical identity

Candidate verbs: grow, bind, root, reinforce, mend, absorb, adapt.

Wood can support control, recovery, agriculture, construction, medicine, or environmental sensing.
It should not become a generic healing affinity with no physical rules.

## Attribute expression proposals

- Vigor: improved derived endurance, recovery, or resistance in registered living/growth actions.
- Insight: plant/environment diagnosis, adaptation, or growth control through relevant skills.
- Might: leverage or restraint from rooted structures, not a universal strength increase.

These are contextual contributions and may carry setup time, resource, terrain, or moisture needs.

## Candidate triggered effect

| Trigger | Effect | Scaling candidates | Resistance/counter | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Successful root/fiber/growth-tagged control action | `slow` | Wood resonance, mastery, Vigor/Focus, available biomass | cutting/fire tools, escape strength, terrain, immunity | Draft |

Slow must specify whether it changes movement distance, initiative, action cost, or another single
readable quantity. Stronger immobilization should be a separate Root/Bind effect rather than an
unbounded Slow stack.

## Environment and civic uses

- cultivation, food/fiber production, living structures, and soil restoration;
- splints, herbal work, grafting, and recovery where a learned skill supports it;
- reinforce or reshape authored wooden structures at material/time cost;
- sense or influence local living growth without creating plants from narration.

## Visual brief

Use fibers under tension, roots following surfaces, leaf/seed motion, sap, cambium, soil displacement,
and visible growth stages. Avoid instant generic vines, green full-body glow, or plant growth with no
biomass/water/time source unless a specific high-tier rule supplies it.

## Open questions

- Is Wood the best player-facing name, or should the affinity be Plant/Growth while wood remains a
  material tag?
- What resources and environments permit growth effects?
- Where is the boundary among Slow, Bind, Root, and terrain creation?
