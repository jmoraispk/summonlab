# Wind

Wiki ID: `affinity.wind`

**Definition:** Motion, severance, and displacement.

**Status:** **Lacerate** — cutting damage scaling with the target's movement.

**Provisional attribute nudge:** A small Agility bonus on level-up. This mild association may be
changed or removed without changing Wind's identity.

**Material language:** Pale green-grey displaced dust, pressure ripples, cloth, hair, vegetation,
particles, and trajectory cues.
