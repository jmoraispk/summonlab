# Wind

Wiki ID: `affinity.wind`

**Definition:** Motion, severance, and displacement.

**Proposed status:** **Lacerate** — cutting damage scaling with the target's movement.

**Matching attribute:** Agility. The candidate nudge is capped at 10% of Agility's contribution; it
is not a direct Agility point on level-up and is not yet applied by production combat.

**Material language:** Pale green-gray displaced dust, pressure ripples, cloth, hair, vegetation,
particles, and trajectory cues.
