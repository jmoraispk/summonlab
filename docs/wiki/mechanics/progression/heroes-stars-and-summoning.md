# Heroes, stars, and summoning

Wiki ID: `mechanics.progression.heroes-stars-summoning`

Design status: **current star rules implemented; summoning-channel probabilities remain authored
content work**.

A summoned hero receives a generated capability profile, personality/history records, a hidden
bounded learning profile, initial skills, and one immutable `1★..7★` rating. The rating is never
upgraded and does not cap level, skills, equipment, intelligence, worth, or future activity.

Stars equal the number of unallocated attribute points earned for each crossed level. See
[Immutable stars](stars.md) for the complete table. Every hero may reach level 999 and awaken at the
same hundred-level boundaries.

Normal, Advanced, or future summon channels may define different probabilities for immutable star
ratings and starting packages. Those odds do not create a native/current-star split or a promotion
economy. Older star-stone, fusion, and star-dependent level-cap proposals are historical research,
not current mechanics.

Affinity is generated separately. Its attribute association is mild and can be broken by later
rules without changing the hero's stars or attributes.
