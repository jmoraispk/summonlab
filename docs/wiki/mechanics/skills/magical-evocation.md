## Evocation — Fire

Requires the **Fire** affinity. A hero of another element may learn these only at heavily reduced potency, if at all. The five Evocation ladders are structurally parallel and thematically distinct.

**Shape.** One line to Rare, then a three-way branch — Great Fireball, Flame Jet and Detonation all descend from Fireball at Expert. Two Fire mages who each mastered Fireball will not have the same mid-game.

| # | Skill | R | T | Requires | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ember** | F | A | Fire affinity Rank I | A small flame — light, a lamp, a lit fire. Utility; negligible damage. **Foundation.** |
| 2 | **Spark** | F | A | Ember Novice | A minor directed burst. Cheap, spammable. |
| 3 | **Warmth** | F | P | Ember Novice | Immunity to cold; a small standing Burn resistance |
| 4 | **Ignite** | F | A | Ember Intermediate | Sets a flammable object or surface alight |
| 5 | **Fireball** | E | A | Intellect threshold, Spark Intermediate, Rank II | The workhorse. Directed projectile, moderate damage, Burn chance. |
| 6 | **Cinder Trail** | E | A | Ignite Expert | Leaves burning ground along a path |
| 7 | **Flame Shroud** | E | P | Warmth Expert, Rank II | A thin burning aura; retaliates against melee attackers |
