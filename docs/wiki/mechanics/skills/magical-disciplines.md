# Magical Skills — Ward, Enchantment, Restoration, and Mind

Wiki ID: `skills.magical-disciplines`

Design status: **draft source import**. These definitions preserve the supplied compendium; exact combat values and Living Village availability require validation.

## Ward

Magical family. Defensive magic, barriers, and dispelling. Skills marked *(elemental)* take the hero's affinity; the rest are element-neutral.

| # | Skill | R | T | Requires | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warding** | F | P | Any affinity Rank I | Small standing magical resistance. **Foundation.** |
| 2 | **Shield Sigil** | F | A | Warding Novice | Absorbs a fixed amount of damage before breaking |
| 3 | **Cleanse** | F | A | Warding Novice | Removes one minor status effect |
| 4 | **Elemental Guard** *(elemental)* | F | P | Warding Intermediate | Standing resistance to the hero's own element |
| 5 | **Barrier** | E | A | Shield Sigil Expert, Rank II | A physical wall of force; blocks movement and projectiles |
| 6 | **Reflect** | E | A | Shield Sigil Expert, Spirit threshold | Returns the next spell to its caster |
| 7 | **Dispel** | E | A | Cleanse Expert, Intellect threshold | Removes an enemy buff or a persistent magical effect |
| 8 | **Counter Sigil** *(elemental)* | E | P | Elemental Guard Master | Attackers of the overcome element take reflected damage |
| 9 | **Aegis Field** | D | A | Barrier Master, Rank II | Area barrier protecting several allies |
| 10 | **Null Zone** | D | A | Dispel Master, Rank II | Magic cannot be cast within the area |
| 11 | **Layered Ward** | D | P | Shield Sigil Master, 10,000 casts | Wards stack rather than replacing one another |
| 12 | **Absorption** | D | P | Reflect Master, Spirit threshold | Damage absorbed by wards is converted into mana |
| 13 | **Sanctuary** | C | St | Aegis Field Master, Rank III | Allies within the area take heavily reduced damage; the hero cannot move |
| 14 | **Unbinding** | C | A | Null Zone Master, Rank III | Strips every magical effect from an area, friendly or otherwise |
| 15 | **Mirror Ward** | C | P | Reflect Master, Layered Ward Expert | All reflected magic returns at increased potency |
| 16 | **The Unbroken Circle** | B | St | Sanctuary Master, Rank IV | Nothing hostile crosses the boundary while the hero holds it |
| 17 | **Severance Ward** | B | A | Unbinding Master, rare catalyst | Permanently removes a target's access to one affinity for the encounter |
| 18 | **Nothing Reaches** | A | St | The Unbroken Circle Master, composite condition | No magic of any kind functions within range except the hero's own |

## Enchantment

Magical family. Buffs on self and others, and the imbuing of objects. Consumes components throughout — the bridge between the magical and craft sectors.

| # | Skill | R | T | Requires | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Imbuing** | F | P | Any affinity Rank I | Enables the application of temporary magical properties. **Foundation.** |
| 2 | **Minor Blessing** | F | A | Imbuing Novice | Small attribute buff on one ally. Consumes a common component. |
| 3 | **Weapon Oil** *(elemental)* | F | C | Imbuing Novice | Coats a weapon in the hero's element for a duration |
| 4 | **Light** | F | A | Imbuing Novice | Persistent light on an object or person |
| 5 | **Greater Blessing** | E | A | Minor Blessing Expert, Rank II | Substantial buff on one ally; longer duration |
| 6 | **Group Blessing** | E | A | Minor Blessing Master | Weaker buff applied to the whole party |
| 7 | **Armour Imbuing** *(elemental)* | E | C | Weapon Oil Expert | Grants armour temporary resistance and a retaliation effect |
| 8 | **Component Craft** | E | C | Imbuing Expert, Alchemy prerequisite | Produces enchanting components rather than buying them |
| 9 | **Sigil Binding** | D | C | Armour Imbuing Master, Forging prerequisite | Applies a permanent enchantment to an item with an open slot |
| 10 | **Empowerment** | D | A | Greater Blessing Master, Spirit threshold | One ally's next skill is cast at greatly increased potency |
| 11 | **Sustained Weave** | D | P | Group Blessing Master, 10,000 casts | Buffs the hero applies last substantially longer |
| 12 | **Elemental Attunement** *(elemental)* | D | A | Weapon Oil Master, Rank III | Grants an ally the hero's affinity temporarily at reduced Resonance |
| 13 | **Greater Sigil** | C | C | Sigil Binding Master, Rank III | Applies enchantments no ordinary slot could hold |
| 14 | **Battle Hymn** | C | St | Sustained Weave Master | Continuous party-wide buff while held; heavy mana drain |
| 15 | **Transference** | C | A | Empowerment Master, Rank III | Moves a buff, or a debuff, from one target to another |
| 16 | **Soul Sigil** | B | C | Greater Sigil Master, rare catalyst | Binds an enchantment to a person rather than an object |
| 17 | **Ascendance** | B | A | Battle Hymn Master, Rank IV | One ally operates at a full mastery tier above their actual for a duration |
| 18 | **The Written World** | A | C | Soul Sigil Master, composite condition | Enchantments applied become permanent and unremovable |

## Restoration

Magical family. Healing, curing, revival. Deliberately scarce at high rarity — the Fabled and Legendary ends of this ladder should be among the rarest things in the game.

| # | Skill | R | T | Requires | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mending** | F | P | Any affinity Rank I, Intellect threshold | Enables magical healing. **Foundation.** |
| 2 | **Close Wound** | F | A | Mending Novice | Small direct heal on one target |
| 3 | **Soothe** | F | A | Mending Novice | Removes pain effects; small mental resistance |
| 4 | **Field Care** | F | P | Mending Intermediate | Non-magical first aid; works without mana |
| 5 | **Regeneration** | E | A | Close Wound Expert, Rank II | Healing over time on one ally |
| 6 | **Cure** | E | A | Soothe Expert, Alchemy prerequisite | Removes poison, disease and Blight effects |
| 7 | **Shared Vigour** | E | A | Field Care Master | Transfers the hero's health to an ally |
| 8 | **Mass Mending** | D | A | Regeneration Master, Rank II | Heals the whole party at reduced potency |
| 9 | **Restore Limb** | D | A | Cure Master, Intellect threshold | Repairs crippling injuries and lasting wounds |
| 10 | **Life Link** | D | P | Shared Vigour Master, Spirit threshold | Damage to a linked ally is partly taken by the hero instead |
| 11 | **Sanctified Ground** | C | A | Mass Mending Master, Rank III | Persistent healing area |
| 12 | **Second Life** | C | A | Life Link Master, Rank III | Revives a recently fallen ally at low health |
| 13 | **Purity** | C | P | Restore Limb Master, 25,000 casts | All cures the hero applies also grant temporary immunity |
| 14 | **Great Renewal** | B | A | Sanctified Ground Master, Rank IV | Restores every ally fully and clears all effects |
| 15 | **Return** | B | A | Second Life Master, rare catalyst | Revives an ally long dead, at cost to the hero |
| 16 | **The Unbroken Thread** | A | P | Return Master, composite condition | While the hero lives, allied deaths are deferred rather than final |
| 17 | **What Was Taken Back** | S | A | The Unbroken Thread Master, composite condition | Undoes an entire engagement's losses. Once. |

## Mind

Magical family. Control, perception, illusion, deception. Rarity climbs fast — low-tier mind magic is deliberately weak, because strong control is the most dangerous thing to hand a player cheaply.

| # | Skill | R | T | Requires | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Presence** | F | P | Spirit threshold | Small mental resistance; awareness of hostile intent. **Foundation.** |
| 2 | **Distract** | F | A | Presence Novice | Briefly draws a single target's attention elsewhere |
| 3 | **Read Surface** | F | A | Presence Novice | Reveals a target's immediate intent |
| 4 | **Minor Illusion** | E | A | Presence Intermediate, Rank II | A small false image or sound |
| 5 | **Fear** | E | A | Distract Expert | Causes a target to retreat briefly |
| 6 | **Veil** | E | A | Minor Illusion Expert | Conceals the hero from ordinary sight |
| 7 | **Confusion** | D | A | Fear Master, Intellect threshold | Target attacks randomly, including allies |
| 8 | **False Terrain** | D | A | Minor Illusion Master, Rank II | An illusion large enough to redirect movement and formation |
| 9 | **Suggestion** | D | A | Read Surface Master, Spirit threshold | A single simple command the target obeys |
| 10 | **Mental Fortress** | D | P | Presence Master, 10,000 casts | Immunity to fear, charm and confusion |
| 11 | **Domination** | C | A | Suggestion Master, Rank III | Full control of one target for a short duration |
| 12 | **Mass Illusion** | C | A | False Terrain Master, Rank III | The whole field appears other than it is |
| 13 | **Memory Edit** | C | A | Confusion Master, Intellect threshold | The target forgets the encounter, or the hero |
| 14 | **The Long Silence** | B | A | Domination Master, Rank IV | Removes a target's ability to act, cast or perceive |
| 15 | **Shared Sight** | B | P | Mental Fortress Master, Mass Illusion Expert | The party sees through every ally's eyes at once |
| 16 | **Unmaking of the Self** | A | A | The Long Silence Master, composite condition | The target forgets every skill they hold for the encounter |
| 17 | **Nothing Was Ever Here** | S | A | Unmaking of the Self Master, composite condition | Every enemy loses all knowledge that the party exists |
