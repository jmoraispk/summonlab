## Family visual grammar

- A bulbous abdomen, eight jointed legs arching around the body, clustered eyes, fangs, and spinnerets remain legible; a humanoid torso is added only from T3 onward.
- Black, charcoal, deep violet, blood-red markings, pale silk white, and green, red, or amber eye accents form the source palette.
- Chitin, coarse bristle, silk, bone, amber, venom sheen, and eye settings remain physically distinct family materials.
- Web decorations, silk robes, egg sacs, wrapped prey, jeweled eye settings, and layered drapery communicate escalation.
- Leg load, spinneret direction, fang posture, silk tension, and eye cluster alignment preserve readable attack and recovery states.

**Escalation axis:** Eye organization, leg span, silk refinement, web topology, and command over brood routes grow from a hunting spider to a cathedral-scale sovereign loom.

**This form's visual delta:** Legs rise like cathedral arches around a layered-web throne, a humanoid torso works immense silk drapery, and a crown of clustered family-native glowing eyes stays bounded to the head.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command a four-arch web cathedral whose silk drapery and lower-form knots reconfigure the arena until each severed throne anchor collapses an arch and simplifies the sovereign loom.

### Teaching goal

Test eye lines, triplines, handed patterns, and brood repair while making every correct cut permanently reduce web topology, leg reach, and command options.

### Signature mechanic

Cathedral Loom: four legs arch like columns around a layered-web throne, each tied to a silk drapery panel and lower-form knot. Gaze calls Crawlers down one aisle, Barb sets Stalkers across another, Hand lets a Weaver cross two aisles, and Brood lets a Broodmother repair a cut. The crown's clustered family-native eye glow identifies one knot, while humanoid hands pull only its drapery. After the learned lower-form pattern, that arch's throne anchor is exposed; severing it collapses the panel, dismisses the lower form, and forces the corresponding giant leg flat. With two arches left, the Queen descends from the throne and can weave only one aisle at a time.

### Telegraph

One crown-eye cluster aligns, its drapery panel tightens behind the humanoid torso, the linked arch rises, and the selected lower form assumes its familiar posture at the knot.

### Counterplay

Use the announced lower-form safe response, reach the exposed throne anchor, and sever it; each collapsed arch permanently removes one command and opens that cathedral aisle.

### Lower-form interaction

Crawler, Stalker, Weaver, and Broodmother each own one silk knot and arch; severing the matching throne anchor dismisses that form and prevents its web rule from returning.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
