Source-draft Elementals are treated as magical entities whose temporary bodies take on unaffiliated spell-matter or one of the four base affinities. This is a visual and encounter-production family, not an accepted claim about origin, life, cognition, agency, personhood, summoning, or hostility. A flame-like outer contour describes shape and motion only; it does not imply Fire affinity.

## Family visual grammar

- Every form is organized around one readable binding seed or core, with no flesh, worn armor, ordinary clothing, handheld weapon, or fixed face.
- The T1 Wisp is a small asymmetric teardrop or flame-like shape without limbs; T2–T5 develop a legless hovering upper body with exactly two coherent arm channels and a tapering lower body.
- Affinity substance forms the complete load-bearing body, limbs, mantle, and directional wake rather than appearing as a colored exterior aura or decorative overlay.
- Form coherence, core structure, and spatial control progress from one wandering Wisp to a four-facet Sovereign while the active telegraph and exposed counterplay remain readable.

**Escalation axis:** Form coherence, core complexity, and control over shaped affinity substance rise from one drifting teardrop to a sovereign that transforms the arena through four learned binding patterns.

**This form's visual delta:** Tall complete hovering figure with exactly two coherent arms, a four-facet central core, four orbital vanes, a tapering lower body, and an affinity-built arena wake.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform the arena through four binding patterns that recombine every lower-form lesson.

### Teaching goal

Test drift destinations, committed tethers, alternating conduits, and mantle gaps as permanent stages of one authored boss encounter.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Transformation — Fourfold Lattice: four core facets successively invoke Trace Drift, Tether Reach, Twin Conduit, and Mantle Orbit; resolving a pattern permanently dims its facet and weakens the final lattice.

### Telegraph

One of four core facets turns outward, its matching orbital vane aligns with the arena, and the lower-form silhouette appears within the selected path.

### Counterplay

Resolve the announced lower-form lesson and strike the outward-facing facet; every dimmed facet removes one pattern from the final transformation.

### Lower-form interaction

Wisp, Elemental Spirit, Elemental Warden, and Elemental Colossus each govern one facet; solving that form's counterplay permanently disables its lattice pattern.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
