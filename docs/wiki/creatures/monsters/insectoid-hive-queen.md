## Family visual grammar

- Segmented chitinous body, mandibled head, compound eyes, four to six limbs, narrow waist, and rear abdomen remain legible across all forms.
- Chitin brown and black mature into glossy crimson, iridescent green-black, and amber accents without replacing caste-specific limb shapes.
- Chitin, resin, wax comb, translucent wing membrane where anatomically present, and amber remain distinct family materials.
- Shell ridging, fluting, resin plating, spiracle vents, and comb-throne structures communicate specialization rather than generic size.
- Wing state is tier-specific: T1–T2 have no wings, T3 has closed wing cases, T4 has functional wings, and the immense T5 Queen is wingless.

**Escalation axis:** Caste specialization, chitin gloss, limb purpose, vent and wing development, and authority over wax-comb routes grow from a worker probe to a stationary production sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Immense segmented wingless abdomen, crown-like head plating, tiny useless limbs, wax-comb throne, four resin valves, and attendants at the base define the sovereign mechanism.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Operate a stationary production boss whose immense wingless abdomen feeds four wax-comb chambers, each generating or modifying a lower-form duty until permanently disconnected.

### Teaching goal

Test resin routes, blade lanes, vent switching, and aerial lattices while each destroyed abdomen valve removes one production branch and opens the throne.

### Signature mechanic

Four Comb Broods: the wingless Queen's immense segmented abdomen connects through four visible resin valves to a wax-comb throne. The Work chamber produces a Worker and resin pellet, Blade hardens a surviving Worker into a Soldier, Breath molts a Soldier into an Elite Drone with closed cases, and Royal opens one Drone's cases into a Royal Guard. Tiny useless limbs cannot defend the valves, so attendants reposition the abdomen toward the selected chamber. Breaking the filled valve permanently collapses that comb and prevents its production. At two chambers the abdomen splits at exposed segment seams and every remaining cycle slows.

### Telegraph

Crown-like head plating turns toward one comb, attendants pull the wingless abdomen, translucent resin fills its valve, and the corresponding lower-form silhouette appears behind wax cells.

### Counterplay

Use the announced lower-form response to reach and rupture the filled valve; each collapsed chamber permanently ends that production path and exposes another abdomen seam.

### Lower-form interaction

Workers, Soldiers, Elite Drones, and Royal Guards are produced or promoted through four separate comb chambers; destroying a chamber ends that lower-form path for the rest of the boss.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
