## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Boars family page](families/boars.md).

**Escalation axis:** A short straight charge becomes a braced line-breaker, curved furrow specialist, formation-opening champion, and three-lane sovereign stampede.

**This form's visual delta:** Compact wild hog with raised bristles, short tusks, low ordinary body, and clean unscarred flanks.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Make one short straight charge that cannot turn after a single scrape.

### Teaching goal

Teach the simplest charge tell and reward a lateral sidestep rather than backward retreat.

### Signature mechanic

Two-Scrape Charge: one forehoof paws twice before the Hog launches along its fixed head line.

### Telegraph

The forehoof scrapes twice and the head lowers between the shoulders.

### Counterplay

Sidestep the fixed lane and punish the exposed flank during its stop.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
