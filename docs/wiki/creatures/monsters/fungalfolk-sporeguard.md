Claims about habitat, communication, society, cognition, agency, personhood, reproduction, rank, and universal behavior are proposals pending taxonomy review. Bioluminescence, spores, mycelium, and damp surroundings are native fungal motifs rather than affinities.

## Family visual grammar

- Cap-headed, stalk-bodied silhouette with gills, bulbous limbs, and a wider top than base remains recognizable.
- Pale fungal flesh and one strong cap tone contrast with bounded cyan or green gill bioluminescence from T2 onward.
- Fungal flesh, spongy cap, mycelium, damp moss, and spore dust remain organic and spatially coherent.
- Cap breadth, gill light, spore density, secondary growth, and ground mycelium escalate at explicit source tiers.

**Escalation axis:** Cap reach and network control progress from a dust puff to battlefield spore writing and finally a sovereign throne connected to the arena floor.

**This form's visual delta:** Wide luminous cap, visible mycelium veins under translucent skin, and the first constant thin shoulder spore cloud.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Qualitatively pivot into a controller that writes inhaling and exhaling lanes through a constant thin spore cloud.

### Teaching goal

Teach that visible mycelium veins and cloud density reveal when a lane draws inward or pushes outward.

### Signature mechanic

Two-Breath Guard: shoulder spores alternately inhale along bright mycelium veins and exhale through the wide luminous cap, reversing safe movement.

### Telegraph

One side's veins brighten under translucent skin as its constant cloud narrows toward the gills.

### Counterplay

Move with the visible breath direction and cut the dim opposite vein during the reversal.

### Lower-form interaction

Mycons synchronize club swings to exhale; breaking their gill rhythm opens the reversing lane.
