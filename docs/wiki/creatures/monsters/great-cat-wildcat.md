A natural feline expedition family centered on concealment, facing, isolation, explosive pounces, and hunting-territory control.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Great Cats family page](families/great-cats.md).

**Escalation axis:** A simple cover pounce becomes high-ground hunting, sightline isolation, saber-tooth pinning, and finally an apex territory hunt that assigns every lower form a role.

**This form's visual delta:** Lean tawny wildcat with long tail, tufted ears, light spotting, and small but readable claws.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Break line of sight once and pounce from the nearest low cover.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to watch cover disturbance and challenge a predictable first ambush.

### Signature mechanic

Nearest-Cover Pounce: the Wildcat always uses the closest valid low cover after breaking sight.

### Telegraph

Its tail tip stops and the chosen cover edge parts immediately before launch.

### Counterplay

Face the opening or step toward it to force an early short pounce, then punish the landing.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
