A natural quadrupedal reptile family centered on shoreline risk, armored facing, clamping, bank drags, death rolls, and floodplain control. It remains distinct from humanoid Lizardfolk and winged Dragons.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Crocodilians family page](families/crocodilians.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single waterline snap becomes a backward clamp, lateral bank-drag specialist, armored death-roll champion, and floodplain sovereign controlling multiple channels.

**This form's visual delta:** Broad armored alligator with high osteoderms, blunt skull, scarred belly seams, and an extremely powerful tail.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Present a scute wall and commit to a fixed-direction death roll that exposes its softer belly at regular intervals.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to read rotational direction and wait for a repeating armor gap rather than attacking the back.

### Signature mechanic

Three-Beat Roll: locked side scutes protect two beats and expose the pale belly fault on the second rotation.

### Telegraph

Side scutes lock together and the tail curves strongly against the coming rotation.

### Counterplay

Evade away from the tail curve and strike the belly fault during the second roll.

### Lower-form interaction

Lower Crocodilians drive targets toward the roll lane but must also avoid its predictable rotating body.
