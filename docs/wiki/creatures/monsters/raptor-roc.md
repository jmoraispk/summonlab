A natural bird-of-prey family centered on vertical tracking, attack shadows, landing windows, carry threats, and airspace control. It remains distinct from humanoid Harpies.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Raptors family page](families/raptors.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single shadow dive becomes alternating perch attacks, silent movement-triggered drops, carry-off pressure, and finally a sovereign that divides the arena into flight bands.

**This form's visual delta:** Vast natural raptor with mountain-eagle anatomy, weathered layered plumage, one damaged outer primary, and an unmistakable full-span silhouette.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Create alternating high, crosswind, and ground-skimming flight bands before a full-span arena dive.

### Teaching goal

Test ground-shadow reading, alternating facing, movement restraint, and landing-route interruption across vertical space.

### Signature mechanic

Three Flight Bands: successive feather rows lift to assign a lower Raptor to one altitude band before the Roc seals the remaining band with its own dive.

### Telegraph

One feather row rises and its assigned bird calls once while the matching air lane gathers debris.

### Counterplay

Clear the assigned bird to create a safe vertical pocket before the Roc commits to the other bands.

### Lower-form interaction

Kite controls the shadow band, Hawk the crossing band, Owl the still-air band, and War Eagle the landing band; defeating one permanently opens its altitude pocket.
