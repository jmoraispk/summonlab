Source-draft Vampires are treated as intelligent blood-drinking undead whose authored encounter hierarchy is expressed through age and refinement, not as a biological race or a universal claim about culture, class, heredity, morality, consent, or personhood. Bestial posture, predatory anatomy, couture, crests, and blood-glass remain visual and mechanical production grammar rather than social canon.

## Family visual grammar

- A tall or lengthening human silhouette retains corpse-white skin, long fingers, fangs, claws, red eyes, and an absence of warm living complexion.
- Posture escalates from feral hunch to impossible stillness; higher forms widen through fitted coats, tailored cloaks, bat membrane, or shadow wings without obscuring humanlike anatomy.
- Black, bone pallor, deep blood red, and aged gold remain paired with velvet, silk, brocade, polished leather, blood-glass, and bat membrane.
- Blood is family-native feeding matter and lineage symbolism rather than Fire or Water affinity by itself; every use has a source, route, vessel, or recoverable feeding window.
- Signet rings, high collars, jeweled clasps, medals, canes, blades, and family crests communicate authored encounter rank while exposing the hand, vessel, or crest that carries each command.

**Escalation axis:** A hungry animal posture becomes a patient hunter, a formal blood-contract Specialist, a centuries-old cloak lord, and finally a crest-commanding throne sovereign whose shattered blood-glass court removes powers one lineage at a time.

**This form's visual delta:** A pale upright hunter in a fitted dark coat with long tails, slicked-back hair, prominent fangs and claws, red eyes, and a slight predatory hunch not yet mastered.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Apply repeatable hunting pressure by pinning a blood mark beneath one coat hem, circling with long claws, then snapping into a fixed inward rake.

### Teaching goal

Teach that refinement means controlled predation: watch which hem is planted, exit on the unweighted side, and attack the overextended claw rather than fleeing the circle.

### Signature mechanic

Hunter's Hem: the Bloodkin steps one dark coat tail onto a blood mark and circles it at arm's length; when slicked-back hair and coat align, the hunter cuts inward with both claws but cannot reverse around the pinned hem.

### Telegraph

One boot and coat tail stop over the red mark, long claws lower to hip height, and the still-hunched shoulders square toward the circle's center.

### Counterplay

Cross behind the pinned coat tail, pull the fight off the blood mark, or strike the weighted boot during the inward rake; the Bloodkin must release the hem before pursuing again.

### Lower-form interaction

Thralls follow the Bloodkin's circular blood scent but collide with its committed inward rake if baited across the pinned hem, turning the shared mark into counterplay.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
