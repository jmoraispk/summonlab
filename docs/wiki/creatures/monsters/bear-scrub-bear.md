A natural ursine expedition family built around mass, committed attacks, grappling, and territorial control.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Bears family page](families/bears.md).

**Escalation axis:** A simple committed swipe becomes frontal maul pressure, an asymmetric hook-and-drag specialist, terrain-breaking dominance, and finally den-scale territorial command.

**This form's visual delta:** Small shaggy bear with a long muzzle, uneven seasonal coat, ordinary claws, and slightly underfed flanks.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Deliver one broad forepaw swipe after standing, then drop heavily onto all fours.

### Teaching goal

Teach the family's basic promise that enormous commitment produces a generous recovery window.

### Signature mechanic

Standing Swipe: the Bear rises, loads one shoulder, and sweeps once before an unavoidable heavy landing.

### Telegraph

Its hips settle and the striking shoulder rises above the other forepaw.

### Counterplay

Move under the raised side and punish the landing recovery from the rear quarter.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
