Source-draft Minotaurs are treated as a horned visual and encounter-production family, not as an accepted biological people or a universal statement about culture, intelligence, rank, blood sport, or hostility. Labyrinth and arena imagery becomes authored battlefield geometry rather than an assumed social identity.

## Family visual grammar

- A bull head, bovine snout and ears, wide horns, vastly overbuilt humanoid torso, digitigrade hooved legs, and thick tail remain legible at every tier.
- Deep russet and black hide, bronze, ox-blood red, and bone define the family palette.
- Coarse hair, horn, bronze, heavy leather harness, chain, and hewn stone remain distinct load-bearing materials.
- Nose rings, horn caps, brands, arena trophies, and chained slabs communicate rank through visible history and repairs.
- Weapon mass and horn spread always preserve a readable commitment direction and recovery side.

**Escalation axis:** Horn spread, visible arena history, and weapon mass escalate from a rangy maul carrier to a sovereign who reconfigures a shattered arena through gateway-wide horn arcs.

**This form's visual delta:** Massive body, horns spreading past the shoulders, chained bronze plate sections, and a greataxe carried one-handed form a mobile labyrinth mechanism.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Qualitatively pivot the fight by dragging chained bronze plate into a temporary labyrinth wall, redirecting movement before striking around its corner.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the Specialist edits routes rather than merely hitting harder: follow chain slack to predict which corridor remains open.

### Signature mechanic

Bronze Turn: the Warrior unhooks one chained plate, plants it as a wall, and pulls the chain around a horn wider than its shoulders to swing the wall ninety degrees; its one-handed greataxe covers only the closing side.

### Telegraph

Chained plate drops from the torso, the chain wraps one broad horn, and the greataxe moves to the side the wall will close.

### Counterplay

Move through the slack-chain corridor, sever the exposed hinge, or cross the non-axe corner while the wall rotates.

### Lower-form interaction

Chargers run the corridor the wall preserves and Bull-kin cover its hinge; breaking the chain collapses both routes into open ground.
