A natural quadrupedal reptile family centered on shoreline risk, armored facing, clamping, bank drags, death rolls, and floodplain control. It remains distinct from humanoid Lizardfolk and winged Dragons.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Crocodilians family page](families/crocodilians.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single waterline snap becomes a backward clamp, lateral bank-drag specialist, armored death-roll champion, and floodplain sovereign controlling multiple channels.

**This form's visual delta:** Ancient gigantic crocodile with broken dorsal ridge, algae-dark hide, wide scarred skull, and a distinct pale belly fault.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Open and close three water channels, route lesser Crocodilians into bank attacks, then cross the arena with a colossal roll.

### Teaching goal

Test wake tracking, clamp anchors, crescent grabs, and roll direction while the arena alternates wet and dry routes.

### Signature mechanic

Three Flood Channels: the Primeval strikes channel gates in order, raising one hunting route and drying another before its crossing roll.

### Telegraph

Its tail strikes the next channel gate and the corresponding waterline rises before any lower form enters.

### Counterplay

Block the lower Crocodilian assigned to that channel, then use the resulting dry bank against the crossing roll.

### Lower-form interaction

Caimans reveal active wakes, River Crocodiles drag along straight banks, Snarejaw controls bends, and the Bastion Alligator occupies the final roll channel; disabling one preserves its dry route.
