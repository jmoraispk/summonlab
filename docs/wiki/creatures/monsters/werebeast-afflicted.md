Source-draft Werebeasts are treated as a staged hybrid-transformation visual and encounter-production family. Claims about human origin, curse, permanence, culture, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- A human frame progressively stretches toward animal proportions while hands, shoulders, legs, muzzle, and tail change at explicit tiers.
- Grey, black, russet, or silver fur remains distinct from torn muted human clothing, hide, iron restraints, claw, and fang.
- Shredded dress, snapped restraints, ritual scars, and restrained moon motifs record the unfinished transformation without defining an affinity.
- Loss of human proportion escalates from patchy changes to an authored sovereign transformation while readable joints remain exposed.

**Escalation axis:** The visible degree and permanence of hybrid transformation advances from an afflicted human probe to a sovereign that commands staged transformations.

**This form's visual delta:** Mostly human frame with patchy fur, wolfish yellow eyes, elongated fingers, and clothing intact but torn at the seams.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe close spacing with one overlong grasp from a mostly human stance.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the patchy forearm tightens before the elongated fingers sweep and leaves the human wrist exposed.

### Signature mechanic

Seam-Split Reach: one sleeve seam tears as the long fingers make a single hooking sweep.

### Telegraph

Wolfish yellow eyes fix on the target while patchy forearm fur rises beneath an intact but straining sleeve.

### Counterplay

Step inside the hooking fingertips and strike the exposed wrist before the sleeve settles.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
