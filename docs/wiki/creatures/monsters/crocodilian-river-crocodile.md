A natural quadrupedal reptile family centered on shoreline risk, armored facing, clamping, bank drags, death rolls, and floodplain control. It remains distinct from humanoid Lizardfolk and winged Dragons.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Crocodilians family page](families/crocodilians.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single waterline snap becomes a backward clamp, lateral bank-drag specialist, armored death-roll champion, and floodplain sovereign controlling multiple channels.

**This form's visual delta:** Mature broad-snout crocodile with heavy dorsal scutes, mud-worn flanks, thick tail, and powerful forequarters.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Lunge a short distance, clamp one target, and drag backward without turning.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to escape the jaw line or attack the planted towing limbs instead of the armored back.

### Signature mechanic

Parallel-Groove Drag: the Crocodile's foreclaws cut two fixed grooves that define its backward towing path.

### Telegraph

The jaw opens low and both foreclaws dig parallel grooves into the bank.

### Counterplay

Sidestep the jaws or strike the planted forelegs during the backward drag.

### Lower-form interaction

Mire Caimans threaten adjacent bank points while the Crocodile commits to its straight drag.
