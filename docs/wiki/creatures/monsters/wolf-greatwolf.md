A natural lupine expedition family built around pack geometry, pursuit, and coordinated prey selection. It remains distinct from transformed humanoid Werebeasts.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Wolves family page](families/wolves.md).

**Escalation axis:** Opportunistic flanking becomes alternating pursuit, directional pack timing, marked-prey command, and finally an arena-scale hunt circle controlled by an apex Greatwolf.

**This form's visual delta:** Monumental but anatomical wolf with a deep mane, long scarred muzzle, distinctive broken upper canine, and arena-command posture.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Tighten an arena-scale hunt circle and assign every lower Wolf form to a readable breach point.

### Teaching goal

Test facing, pursuit swaps, howl direction, and commander separation as one coordinated pack encounter.

### Signature mechanic

Closing Hunt: four breach points contract in sequence while the Greatwolf assigns Grey Wolves to probe, Dire Wolves to pin, the Howler to change tempo, and the Alpha to close the weakest exit.

### Telegraph

Hackles rise in sequence around the circle and the Greatwolf's broken upper canine points toward the next closing breach.

### Counterplay

Defeat or displace the assigned lower form and escape through its opening before the Greatwolf commits to that breach.

### Lower-form interaction

Each lower form owns one hunt-circle function; removing it keeps that breach open and permanently weakens the corresponding boss phase.
