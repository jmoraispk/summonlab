Source-draft Giants are treated as a scale-and-landscape production family rather than a claim that all enormous humanoids share one species, culture, or allegiance. The packet preserves humanlike heavy anatomy, progressive geology, rough mountain materials, and landscape scale while assigning encounter roles through committed weight and terrain displacement.

## Family visual grammar

- Humanlike proportions scaled upward with a heavy brow, thick limbs, dense bone, and a slight stoop that communicates the body's own weight.
- Stone grey, granite blue-grey, earth brown, moss green, glacier white, and warm amber eyes remain the family palette without requiring an elemental origin.
- Rough hide, raw stone, moss, lichen, pine, iron chain, and rope accumulate as bodily geology and oversized practical tools.
- Chains, standing-stone jewelry, skin runes, and whole trees function as ornament at a scale that stays legible from the arena.
- Raw scale and geological coverage escalate together until the Sovereign carries a valley, but every form retains recognizable humanoid load paths and slow committed motion.

**Escalation axis:** Each tier expands both physical scale and the amount of lived-in geology across the body, converting a readable tree-club commitment into slope control, chained-megalith orbit, watershed command, and finally an arena that is itself a walking range.

**This form's visual delta:** A walking mountain range with an inhabited valley across the back, glaciers in the beard, cloud banks at the waist, warm furnace-mouth eyes, and four colossal rune anchors integrated into distinct forests, slopes, standing stones, and watersheds.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Serve as the changing arena itself, carrying a valley that tilts through timber, slope, orbit, and watershed phases while lower Giants operate visible anchors across its back.

### Teaching goal

Test mastery of every scale-reading lesson by asking players to identify the Titan's next weight shift, disable the lower-form crew shaping that phase, and move between stable landscape anchors before the valley changes orientation.

### Signature mechanic

March of the Range: the battle occupies the valley on the Titan's back. At four gait beats, the Titan repeats the family grammar at landscape scale: a forested ridge falls like Timber Fall, a glacier rolls loose like Hill Roll, a chain of standing stones sweeps around a central peak like Megalith Orbit, and shoulder rivers reroute through Three Watersheds. A Brute, Hill Giant, Stone Giant, or Mountain Lord operates the matching rune anchor; disabling that crew and breaking its anchor removes the phase. After two anchors fall, the Titan kneels and rotates the valley ninety degrees, opening new paths through its beard glaciers rather than becoming a larger version of the Champion.

### Telegraph

Clouds shear at the waist, furnace-warm eyes narrow without casting an aura, one valley rune darkens, and the corresponding forest, glacier, standing-stone chain, or river system begins moving several beats before the Titan's foot lands.

### Counterplay

Use the announced landscape route to reach the active lower Giant, defeat or displace it, fracture the exposed rune anchor during the Titan's footfall recovery, and relocate to a surviving standing-stone shelter before the valley rotates; every destroyed anchor permanently removes one gait phase.

### Lower-form interaction

Each lower Giant operates the anchor that enlarges its learned mechanic, and the Titan's phase ends early when that operator is removed; surviving operators relocate when the valley rotates, preserving readable continuity across the boss encounter.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
