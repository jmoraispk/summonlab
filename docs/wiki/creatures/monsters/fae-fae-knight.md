Source-draft Fae are treated as a progressively alien wild-growth spirit visual and encounter-production family. Claims about wild-wood origin, beauty, friendliness, court, culture, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review. Leaf, bark, and branch are ordinary source materials, not a current retired botanical element label.

## Family visual grammar

- A slender weightless-looking silhouette retains layered wings while scale, head growth, and proportion change only at explicit tiers.
- Spring green, petal pink, dusk violet, moth iridescence, and bounded gold pollen light remain source palette rather than affinity evidence.
- Living leaf, petal, spider silk, stained glass, bark, dew, and moth wing remain discrete ordinary or uncanny materials.
- Wing complexity and subtly wrong beauty escalate from a palm sprite to a sovereign whose geometry bends the surrounding frame.

**Escalation axis:** Wing construction, scale, court ornament, and perceptual strangeness progress from a thorn-bearing sprite to a season-transforming sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Human-sized Fae Knight with stained-glass wings, antlers of woven branches, leaf-and-bark armor, and an unnervingly still face.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Qualitatively pivot into a refractive lane controller whose stained-glass wings and branch antlers cast conflicting route signs.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to compare antler shadow with glass color and take the unreflected lane.

### Signature mechanic

False Rose Path: one antler frames a colored wing pane, projecting three apparent thrust lanes while only the pane without an antler reflection carries the leaf-armored charge.

### Telegraph

The unnervingly still face turns last, one woven antler crosses a stained-glass pane, and two reflected lanes brighten.

### Counterplay

Take the pane with no antler reflection and strike the rigid wing rib after the charge passes.

### Lower-form interaction

Sprites fan petals across reflected lanes; cutting their inner seam reveals the true pane early.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
