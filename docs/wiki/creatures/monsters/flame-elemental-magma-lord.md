Source-draft Flame Elementals are treated as a living-combustion visual and encounter-production family, not as accepted claims about origin, life, cognition, agency, personhood, or hostility. Native flame, heat, smoke, scorching, obsidian, and molten rock define family identity and do not automatically constitute the separately engineered Fire-affinity variant.

## Family visual grammar

- A roughly humanoid upper silhouette dissolves into flame above and tapers or hovers below with no feet.
- Temperature remains legible from dull orange through yellow-white to blue-white edges.
- Open flame, ember, smoke, and later source-supported obsidian and molten rock stay materially distinct.
- Core definition and directed combustion progress from a flicker to a sovereign star while heat remains readable rather than overexposed.

**Escalation axis:** Temperature, core definition, and control over combustion rise from one wandering flicker to a sovereign that phases the arena through learned heat states.

**This form's visual delta:** Cracked obsidian shell over living fire, molten rock in fissures, and a mantle of smoke.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control alternating safe lanes by opening source-supported obsidian fissures beneath a smoke mantle.

### Teaching goal

Teach that molten flow selects the next solid shell segment to break.

### Signature mechanic

Fissure Mantle: molten rock routes through three obsidian shell seams, then one segment opens into a sweeping flame gate.

### Telegraph

The smoke mantle lifts and one molten fissure brightens from chest to arm.

### Counterplay

Stand behind the dark shell segment and break its cooling seam after the gate passes.

### Lower-form interaction

Blaze Wardens feed jets into lit fissures; disabling the linked Warden leaves that segment cold.
