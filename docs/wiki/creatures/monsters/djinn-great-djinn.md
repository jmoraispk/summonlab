Source-draft Djinn are treated as a bound smoke-bodied spirit visual and encounter-production family. Claims about air identity, fire identity, spirit identity, binding, wishes, culture, rank, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- A hovering humanoid upper body tapers into smoke instead of legs; solidity and scale change only at explicit tiers.
- Sapphire or violet smoke, warm inner light, gold, brass, turquoise, silk, and gemstones remain distinct source materials.
- Cuffs, rings, headpieces, lamps, and script mark proposed binding status without establishing universal culture or morality.
- The body becomes less solid and more spatially dominant from half-face vapor to a palace-scale throne vortex.

**Escalation axis:** Increasingly coherent command over a smoke body and proposed binding ornaments advances from a small vapor probe to a sovereign lamp-court vortex.

**This form's visual delta:** Giant storm-colored smoke body, jeweled crown, and sacred script written visibly through surrounding vapor.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control alternating script zones through a giant storm-colored body without obscuring the readable binding glyph.

### Teaching goal

Teach that crown direction selects a script and the final open glyph is the interrupt point.

### Signature mechanic

Crowned Clause: the jeweled crown points to one sacred line written in vapor, and the giant body curls around that line until its final glyph closes.

### Telegraph

One crown jewel aligns with the floor while sacred script writes outward from the matching smoke shoulder.

### Counterplay

Cross outside the writing direction and break the final open glyph before the storm body closes around it.

### Lower-form interaction

Djinn ring vectors trace the selected clause; breaking a ring gap leaves its glyph permanently open.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
