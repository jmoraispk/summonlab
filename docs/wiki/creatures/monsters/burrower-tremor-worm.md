## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Burrowers family page](families/burrowers.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single moving ridge becomes a long eruption lane, hookjaw towing specialist, decoy-ridge champion, and persistent tunnel-network sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Thick armored middle rings, vibration feelers, scarred dorsal plates, and a hammer-shaped terminal tail.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Produce several decoy ridges while its heavy body travels beneath one real route, then erupt into a sweeping roll.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to distinguish cosmetic surface disturbance from the physical displacement caused by the true body.

### Signature mechanic

False Tremors: three dust ridges advance, but only the true route moves embedded stones and ends in a body roll.

### Telegraph

False ridges shed loose dust while the real ridge displaces stones from below.

### Counterplay

Track the embedded stones, evade that eruption, and punish the hammer tail left above ground after the roll.

### Lower-form interaction

Lower Burrowers can occupy decoy routes, but their smaller ridge signatures remain distinct from the Tremor Worm's stone displacement.
