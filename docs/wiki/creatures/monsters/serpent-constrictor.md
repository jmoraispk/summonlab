A natural limbless snake family centered on fixed strike range, constriction, venom lanes, buried reach, and living coil control. It remains distinct from sapient humanoid Nagas.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Serpents family page](families/serpents.md).

**Escalation axis:** A fixed one-length strike becomes anchored constriction, a two-lane venom specialist, buried alternating reach, and finally a sovereign whose body coils become arena terrain.

**This form's visual delta:** Long muscular snake with broad gripping belly scales, blunt wedge head, small teeth, and powerful midbody.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Loop around one obstacle or target and tighten only while its head remains anchored.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to distinguish the dangerous body loop from the vulnerable head anchor.

### Signature mechanic

Anchored Coil: a completed tail loop tightens while the jaws maintain one visible anchor point.

### Telegraph

The tail completes a circle and the jaw clamps firmly onto its anchor.

### Counterplay

Break the loop or stagger the anchored head to release all constriction pressure.

### Lower-form interaction

Adders threaten the predictable outside edge while the Constrictor holds the central loop.
