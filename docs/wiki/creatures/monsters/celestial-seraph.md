Source-draft Celestials are treated as winged beings manifested through light and geometric order for authored encounters, not as a biological people or a universal claim about religion, holiness, goodness, authority, moral worth, personhood, or cosmic law. Their light, sunlight colors, halos, and luminous bodies are family-native non-affinity materials and symbols; elemental variants require distinct construction beyond radiance.

## Family visual grammar

- An idealized upright humanoid silhouette remains deliberately symmetrical, with feathered wings and a halo above or behind the head even as the body becomes increasingly abstract.
- White, ivory, pale gold, sunlight yellow, and sky blue remain family-native ordered radiance rather than elemental affinity colors.
- Polished gold, white marble, feather, silk, and source-native pure light stay layered so armor, cloth, wing roots, halo joints, and the suggested body remain readable.
- Halos, ring geometry, sacred script, ribbons, censers, blank ceremonial masks, and wing count communicate rank through exact symmetry and moving parts rather than glow or size alone.
- Escalation increases wing pairs and halo complexity from a simple ring to an orrery, but each tier preserves a breakable alignment joint and a learnable ordered command.

**Escalation axis:** A two-wing Messenger draws one line, a Guardian braces it, a four-wing Warden rotates intersecting axes, a six-wing Archon composes those geometries, and an orrery Seraph commands and sheds entire ordered rings as the arena simplifies.

**This form's visual delta:** A symmetrical being with a body mostly made of bounded family-native light, only a clear humanoid suggestion within, innumerable feathered wings grouped into four readable tiers, and a complex halo operating as a visible four-axle orrery.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command a four-orbit boss arena through layered wings and a working orrery halo, issuing and combining lower-form geometries until destroyed axles permanently reduce both orders and bodily abstraction.

### Teaching goal

Test mastery of straight decrees, braced diameters, intersecting glyphs, and threefold offices while making every successful axle break remove an orbit, wing tier, and phase rule.

### Signature mechanic

Living Orrery: four halo orbits turn around the Seraph's mostly luminous body. The Word orbit calls Messengers to draw radial ribbons; the Guard orbit places Guardians on a diameter; the Warden orbit counter-rotates paired script axes; and the Archon orbit combines two surviving orders under a six-wing marshal. Wings beyond counting are layered into four readable tiers, each bracing one orbit through a visible gold axle. The Seraph covers the active orbit with three wing tiers but must open the matching tier to issue its command. Striking that axle during the learned lower-form safe window destroys the orbit, dismisses its command, and causes one entire wing tier to fold into the body's clearer humanoid outline. After two breaks, the remaining orbits slow and run sequentially rather than together.

### Telegraph

One orrery orbit stops against a gold axle, its matching tier among the many wings opens like measured shutters, sacred script names the selected geometry, and the lower Celestials assume their familiar symmetric positions.

### Counterplay

Read the chosen orbit and lower-form formation, enter its learned safe line or quadrant, then strike the exposed gold axle before the wing tier closes; each destroyed orbit permanently removes a command and makes later phases slower, more sequential, and more humanoid.

### Lower-form interaction

Messengers, Guardians, Wardens, and an Archon each operate one orrery orbit; destroying its axle dismisses that formation, removes the linked wing tier, and prevents the Seraph from issuing the order again.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
