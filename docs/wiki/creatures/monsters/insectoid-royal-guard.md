Hive and caste language becomes authored combat coordination while every biological and cultural claim remains a proposal pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- Segmented chitinous body, mandibled head, compound eyes, four to six limbs, narrow waist, and rear abdomen remain legible across all forms.
- Chitin brown and black mature into glossy crimson, iridescent green-black, and amber accents without replacing caste-specific limb shapes.
- Chitin, resin, wax comb, translucent wing membrane where anatomically present, and amber remain distinct family materials.
- Shell ridging, fluting, resin plating, spiracle vents, and comb-throne structures communicate specialization rather than generic size.
- Wing state is tier-specific: T1–T2 have no wings, T3 has closed wing cases, T4 has functional wings, and the immense T5 Queen is wingless.

**Escalation axis:** Caste specialization, chitin gloss, limb purpose, vent and wing development, and authority over wax-comb routes grow from a worker probe to a stationary production sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Ornate deeply ridged crimson-and-gold shell, translucent functional wings, upright guard posture, and ceremonial spear.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Patrol a three-dimensional lattice, using its first functional wings and ceremonial spear to defend one hive route from above.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to pair wing membrane tension with spear angle, then attack the exposed root after the committed descent.

### Signature mechanic

Royal Lattice: the Guard connects three resin nodes by flying between them, then dives its spear along only the tautest edge; breaking any node slackens that edge and forces a landing.

### Telegraph

Crimson-and-gold shell ridges align, translucent functional wings pull one lattice edge taut, and the ceremonial spear turns along it.

### Counterplay

Destroy the announced node or leave the taut edge, then strike the wing root when the Guard lands to reset the lattice.

### Lower-form interaction

Workers maintain nodes, Soldiers hold ground edges, and Elite Drones reverse one lattice side; removing its node grounds all three duties locally.
