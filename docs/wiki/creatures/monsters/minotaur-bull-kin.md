Source-draft Minotaurs are treated as a horned visual and encounter-production family, not as an accepted biological people or a universal statement about culture, intelligence, rank, blood sport, or hostility. Labyrinth and arena imagery becomes authored battlefield geometry rather than an assumed social identity.

## Family visual grammar

- A bull head, bovine snout and ears, wide horns, vastly overbuilt humanoid torso, digitigrade hooved legs, and thick tail remain legible at every tier.
- Deep russet and black hide, bronze, ox-blood red, and bone define the family palette.
- Coarse hair, horn, bronze, heavy leather harness, chain, and hewn stone remain distinct load-bearing materials.
- Nose rings, horn caps, brands, arena trophies, and chained slabs communicate rank through visible history and repairs.
- Weapon mass and horn spread always preserve a readable commitment direction and recovery side.

**Escalation axis:** Horn spread, visible arena history, and weapon mass escalate from a rangy maul carrier to a sovereign who reconfigures a shattered arena through gateway-wide horn arcs.

**This form's visual delta:** Young and rangy with short stubby horns, rope harness, wooden maul, bare torso, and no armor.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe commitment reading with one rangy overhead maul strike that cannot change direction after the hoof plants.

### Teaching goal

Teach that horn and weapon size matter less than the visible loaded hoof and exposed recovery side.

### Signature mechanic

Young Swing: the Bull-kin drags its wooden maul into a single overhead line after planting one hoof.

### Telegraph

The rope harness tightens, short stubby horns dip, and one unarmored hoof stamps behind the maul head.

### Counterplay

Cross the unloaded side and strike the bare back while the wooden maul rebounds from the floor.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
