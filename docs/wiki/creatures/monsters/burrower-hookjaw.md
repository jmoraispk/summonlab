## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Burrowers family page](families/burrowers.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single moving ridge becomes a long eruption lane, hookjaw towing specialist, decoy-ridge champion, and persistent tunnel-network sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Split hook mandibles, flattened head shield, muscular collar rings, and a narrower towing body with high lateral flexibility.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Catch a target or terrain anchor between split jaws and tow it sideways toward a tunnel.

### Teaching goal

Introduce a rescue and formation-displacement specialist with two separately breakable approach lines.

### Signature mechanic

Converging Hooks: two jaw tips travel under separate cracks, surface around the target, and retract toward the same tunnel mouth.

### Telegraph

Paired ground cracks converge while both hooked jaw tips briefly break the surface.

### Counterplay

Break either convergence line or strike the exposed jaw hinge before the hooks close.

### Lower-form interaction

Soil Grubs crowd the open rescue line while Burrowing Worms threaten the predictable escape endpoint.
