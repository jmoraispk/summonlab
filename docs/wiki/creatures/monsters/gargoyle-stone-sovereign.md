Source-draft Gargoyles are treated as an animated architectural-statue visual and encounter-production family. Claims about origin, night behavior, duty, rank, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- Squat crouched construction, hunched shoulders, horned beast face, clawed grip, and folded architectural wings remain recognizable.
- Sandstone, granite, marble, bronze, mortar, moss, and water staining remain discrete built materials rather than flesh.
- Relief carving, water-spout mouths, restrained amber seams, and pedestal fragments preserve the architectural source grammar.
- Stone quality and wing architecture escalate from useless carved flaps to sovereign buttresses while load-bearing joints stay readable.

**Escalation axis:** Material refinement and wing architecture progress from a perched chipped ornament to a sovereign architecture that commands its own statuary court.

**This form's visual delta:** Architectural-scale body with flying-buttress wings, a throne carved from its own pedestal, and statuary attendants at the base.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command an architectural boss arena where buttress wings, throne quadrants, and statuary attendants restage every learned load path.

### Teaching goal

Test perch chips, hop hinges, relief routes, and tracery braces while each disabled attendant removes one throne support.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Command — Living Elevation: the throne rotates toward one of four statuary attendants, buttress wings frame that quadrant, and the attendant performs a lower-form rule before its pedestal becomes the sovereign's exposed support.

### Telegraph

One buttress wing settles onto the throne, the matching attendant lifts from the base, and familiar amber joints illuminate its source-form posture.

### Counterplay

Answer the attendant's learned tell and break its pedestal bond; each lost support opens a throne quadrant and limits the sovereign's wing span.

### Lower-form interaction

Grotesque, Sentinel, Guardian, and Cathedral Warden attendants each govern one elevation; solving their mechanic exposes the linked throne support.
