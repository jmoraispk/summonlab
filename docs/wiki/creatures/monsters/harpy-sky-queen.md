Source-draft Harpies are treated as a winged visual and encounter-production family, not as an accepted taxonomy or a universal statement about sex, culture, cognition, personhood, social rank, or hostility. Their cliff-flock language becomes readable aerial combat roles while preserving the source anatomy and plumage escalation.

## Family visual grammar

- A humanoid torso remains joined to large raptor wings that replace or extend the arms, backward-jointed bird legs, oversized talons, and a feathered head crest.
- Slate grey, brown, and dirty cream mature into iridescent blue-green, storm purple, and white without losing the family feather pattern.
- Feather, hollow bone, sinew, raw hide, braided hair, and gold ankle ornaments remain physically readable materials.
- Bone jewelry, trophy feathers, braided hair, ankle rings, and crest height communicate status without changing the core wing-and-talon silhouette.
- Every attack keeps the wing roots, talons, crest, and flight direction visible enough to preserve counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Feather quality, talon reach, crest authority, and control of aerial lanes grow from a ragged noise probe into flock formations and a cliff-spanning sovereign storm court.

**This form's visual delta:** Storm-purple and white wings span the cliff arena, the crest forms a full crown, gold cuffs ring every limb, and a family-native non-affinity halo of loose feathers orbits only the physical wing beats.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command a cliff-arena boss across four destructible perches, composing every lower-form lane before successive perch breaks force closer, simpler phases.

### Teaching goal

Test mastery of cry facing, planted hooks, crossing bands, and flock rotation while turning the Queen's enormous wing span into a readable arena mechanism.

### Signature mechanic

Court of Four Cliffs: the Sky Queen spans opposite perches with storm-colored wings and assigns a Screecher, Talon, Skyrender, or Matriarch to each gold-cuffed ledge. One crown feather names the active courtier, whose familiar mechanic establishes a lane; the Queen then closes one wing across it. Destroying the exposed cuff anchor drops that perch, dismisses its courtier, and tears one long primary from the Queen's span. At two remaining perches she must land between commands; at one she loses the loose-feather halo and fights with short committed talon sweeps.

### Telegraph

The full crest-crown raises one named feather, a matching gold cuff rings against its perch, ordinary loose feathers align around the selected lower form, and one enormous wing unfolds across the announced lane.

### Counterplay

Use the selected lower form's learned safe response, then strike the revealed cuff anchor while the spanning wing is committed; every broken perch permanently removes one command and narrows the Queen's reach.

### Lower-form interaction

Each of the four perches binds one Screecher, Talon, Skyrender, or Matriarch command to the Queen; destroying that perch removes the lower form and its associated boss lane for the remainder of the fight.
