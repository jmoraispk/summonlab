**Escalation axis:** A barely held mist learns to drag anchors, weaponize the boundary between wall and room, spread a crowned cloak territory, and finally command a court through hollow regalia whose seals can be permanently opened.

**This form's visual delta:** A sharply defined crowned figure whose robed upper body opens into a broad black-tide cloak, with three geometric crown points, localized frost at condensed anchors, and no feet beneath the spreading hem.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Spread a cloak-shaped territory across the arena, assigning its hem, folds, and crown points as condensation, chain, and remote-edge routes for lower Wraiths.

### Teaching goal

Teach territorial dismantling: the black tide is a mapped cloak rather than generic darkness, and breaking the crown point holding one fold permanently opens that region.

### Signature mechanic

Crown-Tide: three spectral crown points pin the widening cloak to floor and walls. One fold aligns Shade eyes, one carries Haunt chains, and one lets a Wraith borrow its hem as an edge. The Specter must face the active fold, leaving the opposite crown point rimmed in family-native frost and materially condensed.

### Telegraph

The faceless hood turns, one crown point lowers, and a single cloak fold spreads outward as frost traces its hem and lower spirits drift toward it.

### Counterplay

Move off the traced hem, attack the opposite frost-rimmed crown point, and use the opened tear as clear ground; each broken point permanently removes its assigned lower-form route.

### Lower-form interaction

Separate cloak folds accelerate Shade alignment, extend Haunt chain thresholds, and supply Wraith remote edges; breaking a crown point disables the linked fold for every spirit.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
