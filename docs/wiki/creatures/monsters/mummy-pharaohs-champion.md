## Family visual grammar

- A stiff-limbed human or humanoid silhouette remains wrapped from head to foot, with arms held forward, crossed, guarding, inscribing, or commanding through readable linen tension.
- Sun-bleached linen, sand beige, desert ochre, deep gold, lapis blue, and turquoise remain funerary materials and symbols rather than affinity colors.
- Linen bandage, dried flesh, gold leaf, lapis, carnelian, bronze, and sandstone grave goods stay materially distinct and retain visible knots, stitches, glyph routes, and breakable seals.
- Family-native sand and dust follow disturbed wrappings, tomb movement, and burial objects without becoming Earth or Wind affinity by themselves.
- Escalation adds precious thread, glyph density, collars, masks, armor, headdresses, and sarcophagus regalia while the bandages never come off and always explain binding and counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Crude wrapping knots become bronze guard bindings, processional glyph circuits, a masked tomb formation, and a royal sarcophagus engine whose dismantled cartouches remove commands and unwrap a simpler final phase.

**This form's visual delta:** A fully wrapped elite in ceremonial armor with a complete gold death-mask, four visible retaining straps, a broad collar, and family-native tomb sand circling only its stiff footwork.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Assemble lower Mummies into a four-sided tomb formation by turning its gold death-mask toward one side and directing native sand only as a readable footprint record.

### Teaching goal

Teach formation rotation: the mask faces the protected side, the rear strap and ceremonial armor seam carry its weight, and breaking that seam permanently removes one formation wall.

### Signature mechanic

Four-Faced Tomb: the Champion's full death-mask turns toward a cardinal side while ceremonial armor plates unfold along that edge. Husks trail snare strips across one flank, Tomb Guards cross the front, and a Sacred Servant writes a return path behind. Sand disturbed at their feet records every occupied line but has no independent elemental force. The opposite mask strap stretches visibly under the formation load.

### Telegraph

The gold mask rotates with a dry click, one armor edge opens, native sand swirls only around moving feet, and the opposite linen strap pulls away from the wrapped skull.

### Counterplay

Move through the side before the armor edge closes, avoid the familiar lower-form lines, and cut the rear mask strap; each broken strap prevents the Champion from rebuilding that side.

### Lower-form interaction

Husks supply snare flanks, Tomb Guards hold the faced edge, and Sacred Servants inscribe the rear return route; breaking the corresponding mask strap opens all three duties on that side.

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
