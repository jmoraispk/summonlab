A natural bird-of-prey family centered on vertical tracking, attack shadows, landing windows, carry threats, and airspace control. It remains distinct from humanoid Harpies.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Raptors family page](families/raptors.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single shadow dive becomes alternating perch attacks, silent movement-triggered drops, carry-off pressure, and finally a sovereign that divides the arena into flight bands.

**This form's visual delta:** Huge eagle with battered primaries, heavy golden beak, scarred talons, broad wing shoulders, and dark mantle.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Grip and lift one target a short distance while lesser Raptors attack the exposed group below.

### Teaching goal

Teach parties to interrupt a visible lift quickly or use its predictable landing route for a coordinated punish.

### Signature mechanic

Committed Carry: lowered talons grip once, follow one shadow-marked ascent lane, and return through one fixed landing route.

### Telegraph

Its legs lower, primary feathers spread, and the ascent lane lengthens in its shadow.

### Counterplay

Break the grip before lift or attack the predictable landing route when the Eagle returns.

### Lower-form interaction

Lower Raptors pressure the group during the lift but must clear the Eagle's fixed return lane.
