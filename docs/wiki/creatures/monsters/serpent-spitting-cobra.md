A natural limbless snake family centered on fixed strike range, constriction, venom lanes, buried reach, and living coil control. It remains distinct from sapient humanoid Nagas.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Serpents family page](families/serpents.md).

**Escalation axis:** A fixed one-length strike becomes anchored constriction, a two-lane venom specialist, buried alternating reach, and finally a sovereign whose body coils become arena terrain.

**This form's visual delta:** Upright forebody, broad patterned hood, compact rear coil, and visibly distinct paired venom glands.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Paint two visible venom lanes and threaten a conventional strike through the remaining gap.

### Teaching goal

Introduce a ranged specialist that edits escape geometry while preserving a physical throat and hood tell.

### Signature mechanic

Two-Fang Fan: each fang projects one narrow venom line, leaving a deliberate central or outer gap for the follow-up strike.

### Telegraph

Hood spots align with the two lanes and venom gathers separately at both fangs.

### Counterplay

Close behind the hood or bait both spits away from the escape route before crossing.

### Lower-form interaction

Adders guard the chosen gap while Constrictors anchor obstacles that narrow the available lanes.
