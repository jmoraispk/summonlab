A natural bird-of-prey family centered on vertical tracking, attack shadows, landing windows, carry threats, and airspace control. It remains distinct from humanoid Harpies.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Raptors family page](families/raptors.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single shadow dive becomes alternating perch attacks, silent movement-triggered drops, carry-off pressure, and finally a sovereign that divides the arena into flight bands.

**This form's visual delta:** Muscular hawk with banded tail, broader chest, sharp compact wings, strong brow, and larger talons.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Alternate two shallow dives from opposite directions and perch after the second.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to update facing between attacks and recognize a guaranteed grounded recovery.

### Signature mechanic

Paired Passes: the Hawk's tail points toward the next opposite-side pass before both attacks, followed by a fixed perch.

### Telegraph

Its tail fans toward the approach and the primary feathers whistle before each pass.

### Counterplay

Change facing between dives and attack the clearly selected perch after the second.

### Lower-form interaction

Kites cross the ground shadow while the Hawk demands alternating vertical facing.
