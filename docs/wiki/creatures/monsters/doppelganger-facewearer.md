Source-draft Doppelgangers are treated as a copy-quality and leaking-disguise visual and encounter-production family. Claims about infiltration, motive, social behavior, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- A generic humanoid copy silhouette retains one deliberate flaw until layered face and mask motifs appear at explicit upper tiers.
- Clay-grey skin, putty, borrowed colors, porcelain, copied cloth, and smooth flesh remain discrete source materials rather than Earth affinity.
- Seams, wrong joints, ill-fitting clothes, and layered masks reveal copy quality while preserving the readable original-copy relationship.
- Disguise fidelity escalates from an entirely blank true form to a sovereign carrying many stolen-looking faces at once.

**Escalation axis:** Copy fidelity and the number of simultaneous borrowed identities advance from blank smooth flesh to a body-wide sovereign mask procession.

**This form's visual delta:** Flawless copy wearing three layered faces like masks, each shifting to the front in turn.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control three alternating attack rules by rotating layered faces to the front of an otherwise flawless copy.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the rear mask edge moves before the next face and remains vulnerable after the transition.

### Signature mechanic

Front in Turn: three layered faces rotate through the front position, each calling a learned reach, cut, or mirrored response while the vacated edge stays open.

### Telegraph

Two mask profiles slide backward and the third face becomes unnaturally still before its rule begins.

### Counterplay

Read the incoming profile, answer its familiar rule, and strike the trailing mask edge after it rotates away.

### Lower-form interaction

Blank, Halfling Copy, and Mimic each supply one face rule; disabling that lower form leaves its layer unable to close.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
