## Family visual grammar

- Vertical trunk body, root-cluster legs, long branch arms, a bark-suggested face, and canopy remain recognizable.
- Bark brown and grey, moss green, autumn gold, sap, and restrained amber heartwood remain botanical materials.
- Nests, vines, mushrooms, bark runes, scars, and growth rings record age without replacing readable joints.
- Girth, canopy, rooted reach, and resident ecosystems escalate while branch load and root commitment preserve counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Age and ecosystem load turn simple root movement into canopy control and finally a sovereign walking forest that reconfigures the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Ancient hollow trunk with amber heartwood cavity, nesting birds, and mushrooms along the base.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control alternating habitats by opening an amber heartwood cavity that directs birds, mushrooms, and vine screens.

### Teaching goal

Teach that resident life announces which ancient limb is active and leaves the cavity open after command.

### Signature mechanic

Hollow Council: birds leave one branch, non-luminous mushrooms unfurl at one root and catch reflected amber heartwood light rather than emitting light, and a vine screen grows between them before the cavity releases amber sap.

### Telegraph

The hollow trunk opens, amber heartwood pulses, and mushrooms catch reflected amber heartwood light as residents move toward the selected branch-root pair; the mushrooms remain non-luminous.

### Counterplay

Cross outside the marked pair and strike the open heartwood cavity after the sap release.

### Lower-form interaction

Oakheart vine knots stabilize the screen; cutting their braid shortens the command.
