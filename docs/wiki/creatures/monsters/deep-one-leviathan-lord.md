## Family visual grammar

- Hunched long-armed humanoid silhouette, wide flat head, lidless eyes, gill-neck, fins, webbed claws, and needle teeth remain legible.
- Drowned green, deep-sea blue-black, sickly grey, pearlescent white, and bounded cold native light form the source palette.
- Wet scale, eel skin, coral, barnacle, driftwood, rusted metal, whale bone, net, pearl, and shell remain distinct materials.
- Pressure bulk, loss of familiar humanoid proportion, and native light-bearing anatomy escalate at explicit source tiers.

**Escalation axis:** Pressure adaptation and deep-sea command progress from a gasping raider to a sovereign whose court and kraken limbs divide the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Whale-scaled sovereign with kraken limbs, angler-lure crown, net-and-kelp robes, and a court of lesser Deep Ones.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command a four-quadrant court where kraken limbs and a crown of lures stage learned lower-form assaults.

### Teaching goal

Test gill recovery, coral straps, false lures, and organ pressure while each solved court position removes one sovereign limb route.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Command — Fourfold Court: one crown lure names a quadrant, one kraken limb raises its net-and-kelp curtain, and a lower Deep One performs its learned rule; resolving it exposes that limb's sucker anchor.

### Telegraph

One crown lure brightens, the matching whale-scaled kraken limb lifts, and the lesser court assumes a familiar posture below.

### Counterplay

Answer the announced lower-form lesson and break the exposed sucker anchor; each broken anchor drops a curtain and permanently opens that quadrant.

### Lower-form interaction

Fishman, Tidewarrior, Abyssal Hunter, and Deep Priest each occupy one court quadrant; solving its lesson exposes and disables the linked kraken limb.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
