A natural lupine expedition family built around pack geometry, pursuit, and coordinated prey selection. It remains distinct from transformed humanoid Werebeasts.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Wolves family page](families/wolves.md).

**Escalation axis:** Opportunistic flanking becomes alternating pursuit, directional pack timing, marked-prey command, and finally an arena-scale hunt circle controlled by an apex Greatwolf.

**This form's visual delta:** Broad mature wolf with a torn ear, deep neck mantle, dominant upright posture, and accumulated scars rather than armor.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Mark one target, intercept attacks aimed at lesser Wolves, and swap the active pursuit wing after every lunge.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to separate a protective commander from the pack rather than tunneling into its guarded target.

### Signature mechanic

Marked Quarry: a visible scent trail binds the Alpha's intercept to one target until terrain or distance breaks the line.

### Telegraph

Its tail locks level, nose paints a visible scent line, and one foreleg braces toward the marked target.

### Counterplay

Cross strong terrain to break the scent line or bait the intercept away before attacking the lesser Wolves.

### Lower-form interaction

It intercepts for Grey Wolves, completes Dire Wolf pursuit swaps, and changes wings on the Howler's cue.
