Source-draft Doppelgangers are treated as a copy-quality and leaking-disguise visual and encounter-production family. Claims about infiltration, motive, social behavior, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- A generic humanoid copy silhouette retains one deliberate flaw until layered face and mask motifs appear at explicit upper tiers.
- Clay-grey skin, putty, borrowed colors, porcelain, copied cloth, and smooth flesh remain discrete source materials rather than Earth affinity.
- Seams, wrong joints, ill-fitting clothes, and layered masks reveal copy quality while preserving the readable original-copy relationship.
- Disguise fidelity escalates from an entirely blank true form to a sovereign carrying many stolen-looking faces at once.

**Escalation axis:** Copy fidelity and the number of simultaneous borrowed identities advance from blank smooth flesh to a body-wide sovereign mask procession.

**This form's visual delta:** Shifting parade of stolen-looking faces across chest, arms, and back beneath a crown made of masks.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform a copy arena through body-wide face processions while the mask crown commands learned lower-form defects.

### Teaching goal

Test blank wrists, unfinished sides, wrong joints, and rotating mask edges while each removed crown mask strips one copied phase from the body.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Transformation — Parade of Faces: one mask-crown segment lowers, body-wide faces stream from chest to arms and back, and the sovereign copies a lower Doppelganger rule until its source defect reaches the crown latch.

### Telegraph

Borrowed colors move across the smooth flesh, one crown mask tips forward, and the called lower-form defect appears on the last face in the procession.

### Counterplay

Answer the announced copied rule and strike the crown latch when its source defect arrives; each removed mask erases one procession and reveals more blank native flesh.

### Lower-form interaction

Blank, Halfling Copy, Mimic, and Facewearer each define one mask-crown phase; solving its defect exposes the linked latch.
