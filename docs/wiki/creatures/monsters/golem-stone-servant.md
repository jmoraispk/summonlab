Source-draft Golems are treated as a constructed visual and encounter-production family. Statements about servitude, animation, life, free will, makers, rank, and agency are proposals rather than accepted metaphysics or social taxonomy. The packet preserves blocky workmanship progression and readable magical joints without asserting universal personhood.

## Family visual grammar

- Blocky heavy humanoid construction, oversized hands, small or absent head, blank carved face, and visible joint gaps remain legible.
- Clay, fieldstone, iron banding, granite, basalt, marble, gold inlay, and gemstone stay distinct construction materials.
- Runes and seam light remain bounded animation hardware rather than generic affinity spectacle.
- Workmanship and material quality progress from fingerprints and one scratched rune to sculptural relief and a gemstone heart.

**Escalation axis:** Workmanship, material quality, and programmable rune behaviors rise from crude clay to a sovereign monument that reconfigures the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Fieldstone body, iron bands, faint joint seams, blank slab face, and mature baseline proportions.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Hold a short front with fieldstone mass and alternating iron-bound joints.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the active iron band transfers load and leaves the opposite joint open.

### Signature mechanic

Band Brace: one iron binding locks while the opposite stone arm repeats a reliable shove.

### Telegraph

One faint seam brightens and its iron band pulls tight.

### Counterplay

Strike the slack opposite band after the shove to separate two fieldstones.

### Lower-form interaction

Clay Figures attack from the braced side; breaking the band opens both lanes.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
