A natural ursine expedition family built around mass, committed attacks, grappling, and territorial control.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Bears family page](families/bears.md).

**Escalation axis:** A simple committed swipe becomes frontal maul pressure, an asymmetric hook-and-drag specialist, terrain-breaking dominance, and finally den-scale territorial command.

**This form's visual delta:** Old broad-skulled apex bear with silvered mantle, broken claws, landmark pigments in its fur, and immense grounded weight.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Claim three den landmarks and alternate roar, upheaval, and maul phases while commanding lesser Bears around the active landmark.

### Teaching goal

Test committed-swipe windows, guarded fronts, grapple anchors, and terrain collapse as one territorial encounter.

### Signature mechanic

Three Den Marks: the Ancient rubs one shoulder against a landmark to empower the matching lower-form behavior until that mark is broken.

### Telegraph

The marked shoulder sheds fur and pigment onto one landmark before the corresponding phase begins.

### Counterplay

Break the active mark or draw the pack to an unclaimed zone, then punish the Ancient's exposed rubbed shoulder.

### Lower-form interaction

Scrub Bears guard the roar landmark, Cave Bears hold the maul landmark, Hookclaw displaces intruders, and Scarback reshapes the route to the active mark.
