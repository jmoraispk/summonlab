A natural quadrupedal reptile family centered on shoreline risk, armored facing, clamping, bank drags, death rolls, and floodplain control. It remains distinct from humanoid Lizardfolk and winged Dragons.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Crocodilians family page](families/crocodilians.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single waterline snap becomes a backward clamp, lateral bank-drag specialist, armored death-roll champion, and floodplain sovereign controlling multiple channels.

**This form's visual delta:** Long-snouted crocodilian with reinforced jaw hinge, unusual lateral neck flexibility, narrow body, and visible sensory pits.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Sweep an elongated jaw around cover, close behind a target, and drag laterally along the shoreline.

### Teaching goal

Introduce a qualitative lateral grab that rewards moving toward the hinge rather than away from the teeth.

### Signature mechanic

Crescent Snare: the far jaw tip traces a semicircle around cover before the hinge closes and drags sideways.

### Telegraph

The far jaw tip taps the ground and the flexible neck bends into a visible crescent.

### Counterplay

Step toward the head hinge or strike the narrow jaw side before closure.

### Lower-form interaction

Caimans occupy nearby water exits while River Crocodiles threaten the straight retreat from the crescent.
