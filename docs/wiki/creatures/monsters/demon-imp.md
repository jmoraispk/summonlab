Source-draft Demons are treated as physically manifested underworld spirits organized into an authored hostile hierarchy, not as a biological people or a universal claim about religion, evil, culture, cognition, personhood, or social worth. Their source-native embers, magma, smoke, live fire, and obsidian are infernal body materials and non-affinity rank signs; Fire affinity must add a distinct engineered combustion grammar rather than duplicate that identity.

## Family visual grammar

- A horned head, broadening shoulders, digitigrade or hooved legs, long tail, claws, and bat-like wing membranes remain legible from cat-sized pest to enthroned sovereign.
- Blood red or ashen hide, char black, source-native ember orange, molten yellow, and obsidian preserve an infernal body whose internal fire is explained anatomy rather than affinity by default.
- Charred hide, obsidian, blackened iron, native molten rock, smoke, and bat membrane retain distinct surfaces instead of collapsing into a generic flame silhouette.
- Brands, infernal sigils, chains, spiked black armor, skull motifs, and horn arrangements communicate authored rank and command routes without implying a universal moral taxonomy.
- Horn count, wing authority, and the proportion of source-native fire or obsidian increase with rank, but every form preserves visible hide, joints, brand routes, and an interruptible hierarchy anchor.

**Escalation axis:** A pest steals brands, a line Fiend spends them, a plated Hellknight sentences targets through a seal, an Archfiend chains the hierarchy into formation, and a throne-bound Prince promotes legions until broken command brands strip away its corona and court.

**This form's visual delta:** A cat-sized red pest with pinprick horns, small bat wings, needle teeth, clawed feet, and a whip-thin tail shaped to carry one visible brand token.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe target awareness by fluttering behind a marked combatant, stealing one visible infernal brand token with its tail, and becoming bolder only while carrying it.

### Teaching goal

Teach that infernal power is routed through hierarchy marks: deny the theft, strike the tail-held token, and turn the Imp's tiny rank against higher forms.

### Signature mechanic

Petty Theft: the Imp's whip tail hooks a loose brand shed by another Demon's attack or a marked arena object; while carrying it, the cat-sized pest can make one needle-tooth dive, but the token swings visibly behind and drops on any interruption.

### Telegraph

Pinprick horns angle back, small bat wings flutter in place, and the whip-thin tail circles one dark-red brand before darting for it.

### Counterplay

Move between the Imp and brand, hit the swinging tail during its straight dive, or collect the dropped token before another Demon can use it.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
