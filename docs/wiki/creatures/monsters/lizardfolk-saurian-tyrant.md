## Family visual grammar

- An upright forward-leaning reptilian body keeps clawed hands and feet, a long counterbalancing tail, a crocodilian or iguana-like head, and an escalating dorsal ridge.
- Swamp green, mud brown, moss, and ochre are punctuated by warning-bright orange, red, or yellow throat and crest colors.
- Scale, bone, obsidian, hide, river reed, ordinary mud, and feather remain separately legible materials.
- Paint, bone piercings, trophy skulls, woven shields, and feather headdresses accumulate without obscuring natural plating.
- Tail angle, crest posture, dorsal plates, hands, and planted feet preserve the direction and recovery of each attack.

**Escalation axis:** Crest height, dorsal spines, natural plating, tail weaponization, and control of marked hunting ground grow from a lean scout to a sovereign back-sail war court.

**This form's visual delta:** A full back-sail crest, ceremonial feather-and-hide mantle, stone tail-club, and war paint across the entire plated body create an unmistakable sovereign mechanism.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform a five-mark hunting ground through a full back-sail crest, ordering lower forms until each cut mantle tie lowers one sail section and removes a phase rule.

### Teaching goal

Test tail direction, shield weave, spine gates, and trophy quarters while permanent sail collapse turns a formation boss into a readable grounded duel.

### Signature mechanic

Five Marks of the Sail: five painted panels on the full back-sail map to arena markings. Bone calls Scouts across one line, Reed braces Warriors, Obsidian raises a Spineback gate, Skull orders a Chieftain quarter, and Stone commits the Tyrant's own tail-club through the surviving marks. The ceremonial feather-and-hide mantle is tied once beneath each panel. After the selected learned pattern resolves, its tie stretches across the dorsal gap; cutting it folds that sail panel, erases the mark, and removes the command. With two panels left, the mantle falls and the Tyrant must plant before every tail sweep.

### Telegraph

One body-painted sail panel rises, the matching mantle tie pulls taut, its arena mark is exposed, and the chosen lower form assumes its known posture.

### Counterplay

Use the selected lower-form response, reach the exposed dorsal tie, and cut it; every collapsed panel permanently removes one command and opens that section of the arena.

### Lower-form interaction

Scout, Warrior, Spineback, and Swamp Chieftain each occupy a painted sail mark; cutting that panel's tie ends the linked lower-form pattern for all later phases.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
