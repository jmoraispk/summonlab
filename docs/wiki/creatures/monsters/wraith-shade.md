Source-draft Wraiths are treated as hostile incorporeal dead-person spirits for encounter production, not as a biological species or a universal statement about the dead, memory, autonomy, morality, or afterlife. Cold light, frost, and chill are family-native signs of deathly condensation in the source and remain explicitly non-affinity unless an authored elemental construction changes material and behavior.

## Family visual grammar

- A hooded or robed humanoid upper body becomes progressively defined while the lower body always trails into smoke or cold vapor without visible feet.
- Black, charcoal, deep indigo, and ash grey carry one cold eye accent in pale blue, white, or sickly green; this family-native cold light is not an elemental affinity by itself.
- Smoke, tattered shroud-cloth, cold vapor, rusted chain, and hollow armor preserve incorporeality through negative space and incomplete edges.
- Chains, funeral shrouds, spectral crowns, and localized rings of source-native frost become anchors that explain when and where an otherwise incorporeal form can be opposed.
- Escalation condenses a faint outline into a sharply defined regal silhouette while retaining a vapor tail, empty interior, and one readable anchor for every phase-through action.

**Escalation axis:** A barely held mist learns to drag anchors, weaponize the boundary between wall and room, spread a crowned cloak territory, and finally command a court through hollow regalia whose seals can be permanently opened.

**This form's visual delta:** A faint smear of ash-grey cold vapor with two pale points of light and only the suggestion of a hooded humanoid upper body, never feet.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe recognition by fading to a low smear of vapor, then condensing around two cold eye points for one short life-draining reach.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the eyes are both warning and anchor: the Shade can be harmed only as the second point steadies and its humanoid outline becomes readable.

### Signature mechanic

Held Look: one cold point fixes on a target while the second drifts through the fog; when the two align, a faint shoulder-and-hand outline condenses for a single touch before smearing away again.

### Telegraph

The wandering point slows, the two lights level like eyes, and nearby vapor draws inward to sketch one raised shoulder and hand.

### Counterplay

Move across the drifting point to delay alignment, then strike between the two lights during the brief complete outline rather than attacking the surrounding fog.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
