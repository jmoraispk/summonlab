## Family visual grammar

- Squat crouched construction, hunched shoulders, horned beast face, clawed grip, and folded architectural wings remain recognizable.
- Sandstone, granite, marble, bronze, mortar, moss, and water staining remain discrete built materials rather than flesh.
- Relief carving, water-spout mouths, restrained amber seams, and pedestal fragments preserve the architectural source grammar.
- Stone quality and wing architecture escalate from useless carved flaps to sovereign buttresses while load-bearing joints stay readable.

**Escalation axis:** Material refinement and wing architecture progress from a perched chipped ornament to a sovereign architecture that commands its own statuary court.

**This form's visual delta:** Crouched granite body, weathered moss streaks, small bat wings, blunt claws, and clear masonry joints.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Repeat short wall-to-floor hops that anchor a predictable claw line.

### Teaching goal

Teach that moss compression and the small wing fold announce the landing claw.

### Signature mechanic

Mossed Hop: granite legs push from a wall while small bat wings stabilize, never sustain, one blunt-claw landing.

### Telegraph

Weathered moss squeezes from one shoulder and the matching small wing unfolds halfway.

### Counterplay

Cross under the unwinged side and punish the mortar gap at the landing shoulder.

### Lower-form interaction

Grotesques drop into the Sentinel's landing lane; breaking its shoulder gap makes both land short.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
