## Family visual grammar

- Segmented chitinous body, mandibled head, compound eyes, four to six limbs, narrow waist, and rear abdomen remain legible across all forms.
- Chitin brown and black mature into glossy crimson, iridescent green-black, and amber accents without replacing caste-specific limb shapes.
- Chitin, resin, wax comb, translucent wing membrane where anatomically present, and amber remain distinct family materials.
- Shell ridging, fluting, resin plating, spiracle vents, and comb-throne structures communicate specialization rather than generic size.
- Wing state is tier-specific: T1–T2 have no wings, T3 has closed wing cases, T4 has functional wings, and the immense T5 Queen is wingless.

**Escalation axis:** Caste specialization, chitin gloss, limb purpose, vent and wing development, and authority over wax-comb routes grow from a worker probe to a stationary production sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Darker hardened carapace, enlarged mandibles, upright stance, forelimbs elongated into natural blades, and explicitly no wings.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Hold a repeatable hive lane by bracing natural forelimb blades along an existing resin route.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the darker wingless Soldier commits both blade limbs to a marked path and exposes its narrow waist from the side.

### Signature mechanic

Route Shears: the Soldier follows one resin trail, plants behind its larger mandibles, and closes elongated forelimbs across the path like shears.

### Telegraph

Hardened carapace lowers, both natural blades open around the resin line, and the narrow waist twists square to it.

### Counterplay

Leave the marked route, attack the exposed waist from the side, or erase the resin node to end the guided close.

### Lower-form interaction

Workers extend routes for Soldiers; destroying the active pellet removes the Soldier's guided lane and forces ordinary movement.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
