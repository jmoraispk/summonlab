## Family visual grammar

- Hunched long-armed humanoid silhouette, wide flat head, lidless eyes, gill-neck, fins, webbed claws, and needle teeth remain legible.
- Drowned green, deep-sea blue-black, sickly grey, pearlescent white, and bounded cold native light form the source palette.
- Wet scale, eel skin, coral, barnacle, driftwood, rusted metal, whale bone, net, pearl, and shell remain distinct materials.
- Pressure bulk, loss of familiar humanoid proportion, and native light-bearing anatomy escalate at explicit source tiers.

**Escalation axis:** Pressure adaptation and deep-sea command progress from a gasping raider to a sovereign whose court and kraken limbs divide the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Finned forearms and calves, coral-shard chest armor, webbed claws, and a barbed spear.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Repeat spear pressure while coral chest armor protects one predictable approach.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the armor strap and fin flare reveal the spear's committed side.

### Signature mechanic

Barbed Tide Line: the spear hooks a lane while the opposite finned forearm guards behind coral shards.

### Telegraph

Forearm and calf fins flare, one coral strap pulls tight, and the barbed spear lowers.

### Counterplay

Cross the unarmored side and cut the taut coral strap after the spear passes.

### Lower-form interaction

Fishmen rush along the spear lane; cutting the strap exposes both their gill recoveries.
