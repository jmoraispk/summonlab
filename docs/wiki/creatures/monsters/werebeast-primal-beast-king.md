## Family visual grammar

- A human frame progressively stretches toward animal proportions while hands, shoulders, legs, muzzle, and tail change at explicit tiers.
- Grey, black, russet, or silver fur remains distinct from torn muted human clothing, hide, iron restraints, claw, and fang.
- Shredded dress, snapped restraints, ritual scars, and restrained moon motifs record the unfinished transformation without defining an affinity.
- Loss of human proportion escalates from patchy changes to an authored sovereign transformation while readable joints remain exposed.

**Escalation axis:** The visible degree and permanence of hybrid transformation advances from an afflicted human probe to a sovereign that commands staged transformations.

**This form's visual delta:** Dire-scaled bulk, storm-like mane, horn-and-antler crown grown from the skull, and shattered binding runes floating around the body.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform a four-quadrant hunting arena by shattering one binding rune and commanding a learned lower-form hunt in each phase.

### Teaching goal

Test reach, pounce lanes, hock pivots, and collar openings while each solved hunt restores one restraint to the sovereign.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Transformation — Four Broken Bindings: the horn-and-antler crown points to one floating rune, the rune shatters, and an Afflicted, Half-turned, Werebeast, or Alpha performs its learned hunt before the King's matching limb fully transforms.

### Telegraph

One crown tine aligns with a binding rune, its fracture travels toward the matching limb, and the lower form assumes its familiar starting posture.

### Counterplay

Solve the announced lower-form lesson, then strike the exposed crown root before the limb locks; each restored binding removes one hunt and reduces the final transformation.

### Lower-form interaction

Afflicted, Half-turned, Werebeast, and Alpha each author one transformation phase; defeating or disrupting that form exposes the linked crown root.
