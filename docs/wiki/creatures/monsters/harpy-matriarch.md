Source-draft Harpies are treated as a winged visual and encounter-production family, not as an accepted taxonomy or a universal statement about sex, culture, cognition, personhood, social rank, or hostility. Their cliff-flock language becomes readable aerial combat roles while preserving the source anatomy and plumage escalation.

## Family visual grammar

- A humanoid torso remains joined to large raptor wings that replace or extend the arms, backward-jointed bird legs, oversized talons, and a feathered head crest.
- Slate grey, brown, and dirty cream mature into iridescent blue-green, storm purple, and white without losing the family feather pattern.
- Feather, hollow bone, sinew, raw hide, braided hair, and gold ankle ornaments remain physically readable materials.
- Bone jewelry, trophy feathers, braided hair, ankle rings, and crest height communicate status without changing the core wing-and-talon silhouette.
- Every attack keeps the wing roots, talons, crest, and flight direction visible enough to preserve counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Feather quality, talon reach, crest authority, and control of aerial lanes grow from a ragged noise probe into flock formations and a cliff-spanning sovereign storm court.

**This form's visual delta:** Ceremonial plumage forms a cloak around vivid wings, gold ankle rings cap the talons, the crest becomes elaborate, and hair is braided with trophy feathers.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Marshal a rotating flock formation, protecting one lower flyer with ceremonial plumage while attacking from the opposite edge.

### Teaching goal

Teach formation rotation: read the gold ankle ring that owns the current guard and punish the uncovered trailing wing.

### Signature mechanic

Ceremonial Mantle: the Matriarch wraps cloak-like plumage around one lower Harpy, then rotates the flock one quarter-turn and attacks from the open side with the opposite talon.

### Telegraph

Its elaborate crest leans toward the protected flyer, one gold ankle ring lifts, and braided trophy feathers sweep around that side.

### Counterplay

Move opposite the raised ring, separate the sheltered flyer, or strike the trailing wing before the rotation completes.

### Lower-form interaction

It shelters Screechers, Talons, or a Skyrender in turn; each uses its learned lane from the protected edge, so removing that lower form shortens the rotation.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
