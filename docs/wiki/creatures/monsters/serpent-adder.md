A natural limbless snake family centered on fixed strike range, constriction, venom lanes, buried reach, and living coil control. It remains distinct from sapient humanoid Nagas.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Serpents family page](families/serpents.md).

**Escalation axis:** A fixed one-length strike becomes anchored constriction, a two-lane venom specialist, buried alternating reach, and finally a sovereign whose body coils become arena terrain.

**This form's visual delta:** Small thick-bodied viper with zigzag markings, short fangs, keeled scales, and a compact tail.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Coil once and strike exactly one body length.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to estimate a fixed biological strike range from the visible coil.

### Signature mechanic

One-Length Strike: the Adder's extension never exceeds the length stored in its single tight coil.

### Telegraph

Its neck forms a tight S and the forked tongue stops flicking.

### Counterplay

Step beyond the fixed reach and punish the fully extended neck.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
