Source-draft Mummies are treated as ritually preserved, linen-wrapped dead whose grave goods and offices supply authored encounter ranks, not as a biological people or evidence about any real culture, religion, ethnicity, divinity, royal legitimacy, morality, or afterlife. Sand and dust are family-native burial residue and non-affinity staging; true affinities require separate material and behavior construction.

## Family visual grammar

- A stiff-limbed human or humanoid silhouette remains wrapped from head to foot, with arms held forward, crossed, guarding, inscribing, or commanding through readable linen tension.
- Sun-bleached linen, sand beige, desert ochre, deep gold, lapis blue, and turquoise remain funerary materials and symbols rather than affinity colors.
- Linen bandage, dried flesh, gold leaf, lapis, carnelian, bronze, and sandstone grave goods stay materially distinct and retain visible knots, stitches, glyph routes, and breakable seals.
- Family-native sand and dust follow disturbed wrappings, tomb movement, and burial objects without becoming Earth or Wind affinity by themselves.
- Escalation adds precious thread, glyph density, collars, masks, armor, headdresses, and sarcophagus regalia while the bandages never come off and always explain binding and counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Crude wrapping knots become bronze guard bindings, processional glyph circuits, a masked tomb formation, and a royal sarcophagus engine whose dismantled cartouches remove commands and unwrap a simpler final phase.

**This form's visual delta:** A fully wrapped ritual attendant with gilded bands layered over linen, a jeweled pectoral collar with an opening front clasp, and three long glyph-inscribed strips controlled by stiff fingers.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Qualitatively pivot the encounter into ordered route control by unspooling three gilded glyph bands that record movement and then rewind along the same processional sequence.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to read glyph order, deliberately cross one strip to create an escape break, and interrupt the collar clasp before the recorded path pulls combatants backward.

### Signature mechanic

Written Procession: the Servant extends three gilded bands from its jeweled collar, touching floor points in a clearly numbered glyph order. After the third point, the bands tighten in reverse and drag anyone standing on a recorded segment back toward the previous point. Crossing two bands before completion tangles them; striking the open collar clasp cancels the rewind.

### Telegraph

The jeweled collar opens at one front clasp, faint family-native glyph light travels one-two-three along separate bands, and the Servant's stiff hands cross before the order reverses.

### Counterplay

Track the numbered glow, leave the latest segment, cross two loose bands to tangle them, or strike the exposed collar clasp before the hands complete their cross.

### Lower-form interaction

Husks and Tomb Guards are pulled backward along the same recorded sequence to renew snares or thresholds; tangling the bands displaces those lower forms and interrupts every rewind.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
