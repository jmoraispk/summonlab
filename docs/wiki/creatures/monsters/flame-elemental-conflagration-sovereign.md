Source-draft Flame Elementals are treated as a living-combustion visual and encounter-production family, not as accepted claims about origin, life, cognition, agency, personhood, or hostility. Native flame, heat, smoke, scorching, obsidian, and molten rock define family identity and do not automatically constitute the separately engineered Fire-affinity variant.

## Family visual grammar

- A roughly humanoid upper silhouette dissolves into flame above and tapers or hovers below with no feet.
- Temperature remains legible from dull orange through yellow-white to blue-white edges.
- Open flame, ember, smoke, and later source-supported obsidian and molten rock stay materially distinct.
- Core definition and directed combustion progress from a flicker to a sovereign star while heat remains readable rather than overexposed.

**Escalation axis:** Temperature, core definition, and control over combustion rise from one wandering flicker to a sovereign that phases the arena through learned heat states.

**This form's visual delta:** Blue-white chest star, whole-body solar corona, standing flame crown, and a source-native scorched ground ring.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform the arena through dull, white-hot, and blue-white phases that recombine every lower-form heat lesson.

### Teaching goal

Test cinder endpoints, smoke commitments, jet lanes, and fissure cooling without reducing the boss to brighter fire.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Transformation — Three Temperatures: the chest star shifts the scorched ring through Cinder Drift, Twin Jet, and Fissure Mantle phases; completing each learned response extinguishes one segment of the solar corona and lowers the standing flame crown.

### Telegraph

The blue-white star changes edge color, the corona divides into the selected pattern, and one crown tine bends toward the active lower form.

### Counterplay

Resolve the announced lower-form pattern, then strike the dimmed star edge; each success permanently removes one corona segment and shortens the final cycle.

### Lower-form interaction

Wisps mark endpoints, Blaze Wardens define jets, and Magma Lords hold fissure segments; each defeated contributor removes its phase amplifier.
