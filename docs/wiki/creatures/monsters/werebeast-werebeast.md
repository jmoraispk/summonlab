## Family visual grammar

- A human frame progressively stretches toward animal proportions while hands, shoulders, legs, muzzle, and tail change at explicit tiers.
- Grey, black, russet, or silver fur remains distinct from torn muted human clothing, hide, iron restraints, claw, and fang.
- Shredded dress, snapped restraints, ritual scars, and restrained moon motifs record the unfinished transformation without defining an affinity.
- Loss of human proportion escalates from patchy changes to an authored sovereign transformation while readable joints remain exposed.

**Escalation axis:** The visible degree and permanence of hybrid transformation advances from an afflicted human probe to a sovereign that commands staged transformations.

**This form's visual delta:** Full anthropomorphic beast with thick pelt, digitigrade legs, heavy claws, fangs, and only a belt and rag left of human dress.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Qualitatively pivot into a mobile hunter that crosses two claw lanes from different digitigrade footholds.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to read hock compression, leave the second lane, and break the belt pivot rather than chase speed.

### Signature mechanic

Hock Crosscut: one digitigrade leg plants for a low claw lane, then the opposite hock snaps the full beast through a crossing high lane.

### Telegraph

The thick pelt parts over one compressed hock while the ragged belt twists opposite the raised claws.

### Counterplay

Cross behind the first planted hock and sever or stagger the belt pivot before the second claw lane resolves.

### Lower-form interaction

Half-turned pounces herd targets into the first cut; interrupting their recovery leaves the crosscut without its setup.
