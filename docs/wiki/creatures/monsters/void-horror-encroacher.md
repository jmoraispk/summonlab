## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Void Horrors family page](families/void-horrors.md).

**Escalation axis:** Coherence is progressively subtracted from a thin-limbed crawler until the sovereign is an unresolved world-shaped absence with one readable eye-ring system.

**This form's visual delta:** Vast shape only partly present, edges dissolving into negative space, and limbs that end before they end.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control alternating arena margins with a vast form only partly present and negative limbs that stop before their endpoints.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the dissolving edge nearest the sharpest star gap is the only committed margin.

### Signature mechanic

Before-It-Ends: one negative limb begins beyond the frame, stops before it ends, and subtracts a lane until the adjacent partial edge returns.

### Telegraph

A vast contour enters only part of the scene while one cold accent marks the partial edge that will return last.

### Counterplay

Move through the unmarked negative limb and strike the returning partial edge at its cold accent.

### Lower-form interaction

Fractures fold apparent exits toward the selected margin; breaking their hinge restores one lane.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
