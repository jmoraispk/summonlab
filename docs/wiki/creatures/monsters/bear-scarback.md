## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Bears family page](families/bears.md).

**Escalation axis:** A simple committed swipe becomes frontal maul pressure, an asymmetric hook-and-drag specialist, terrain-breaking dominance, and finally den-scale territorial command.

**This form's visual delta:** Huge scarred bear with splintered debris caught in its coat and a recognizable pale scar across the back.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Bring down a tree, pillar, or boulder as moving cover before charging through the debris.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to read the environment as part of the Bear's commitment and escape before the route closes.

### Signature mechanic

Falling Territory: Scarback tests one structure, topples it into a fixed lane, then follows through the debris.

### Telegraph

Its broad back scars stretch while both forepaws test the chosen structure.

### Counterplay

Leave the fall lane or attack the planted hind legs before the structure gives way.

### Lower-form interaction

Lower Bears use the fallen structure as flank cover, but lose that advantage if the collapse is interrupted.
