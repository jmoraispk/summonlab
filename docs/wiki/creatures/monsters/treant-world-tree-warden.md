## Family visual grammar

- Vertical trunk body, root-cluster legs, long branch arms, a bark-suggested face, and canopy remain recognizable.
- Bark brown and grey, moss green, autumn gold, sap, and restrained amber heartwood remain botanical materials.
- Nests, vines, mushrooms, bark runes, scars, and growth rings record age without replacing readable joints.
- Girth, canopy, rooted reach, and resident ecosystems escalate while branch load and root commitment preserve counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Age and ecosystem load turn simple root movement into canopy control and finally a sovereign walking forest that reconfigures the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Walking forest with a waterfall down its back, boulders caught in roots, sky-filling canopy, and entire groves on its shoulders.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Transform a four-grove arena by rerouting a back waterfall through boulder-bound roots and commanding learned lower-form habitats.

### Teaching goal

Test root planting, canopy loads, vine lanes, and heartwood timing while every solved grove becomes permanently traversable.

### Signature mechanic

Arena Transformation — Four Seasonal Groves: the waterfall feeds one shoulder grove, boulders lock its root arch, and a lower form performs its learned rule there; resolving it exposes the supply root and drains that grove into a safe path.

### Telegraph

Waterfall flow bends toward one shoulder grove, its caught boulders grind, and the matching lower-form marker appears beneath the sky-filling canopy.

### Counterplay

Answer the called lower-form lesson, then sever the exposed supply root; each drained grove removes one command and opens the sovereign trunk.

### Lower-form interaction

Sapling-walker, Young Treant, Oakheart, and Elder Warden each govern one grove; solving that form's lesson permanently drains its route.
