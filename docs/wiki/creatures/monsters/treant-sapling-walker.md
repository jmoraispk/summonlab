## Family visual grammar

- Vertical trunk body, root-cluster legs, long branch arms, a bark-suggested face, and canopy remain recognizable.
- Bark brown and grey, moss green, autumn gold, sap, and restrained amber heartwood remain botanical materials.
- Nests, vines, mushrooms, bark runes, scars, and growth rings record age without replacing readable joints.
- Girth, canopy, rooted reach, and resident ecosystems escalate while branch load and root commitment preserve counterplay.

**Escalation axis:** Age and ecosystem load turn simple root movement into canopy control and finally a sovereign walking forest that reconfigures the arena.

**This form's visual delta:** Thin whip-like trunk, few leaves, shallow root feet, and a face barely suggested in bark.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe close footwork with one shallow-root lash.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the thin trunk must plant one shallow root before a branch can whip.

### Signature mechanic

Shallow Plant: one root foot spreads and the opposite whip-like branch sweeps its narrow arc.

### Telegraph

Few leaves turn edge-on while one shallow root fans across the ground.

### Counterplay

Cross behind the planted root and strike the thin trunk before it pulls free.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.

- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.

- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
