## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Boars family page](families/boars.md).

**Escalation axis:** A short straight charge becomes a braced line-breaker, curved furrow specialist, formation-opening champion, and three-lane sovereign stampede.

**This form's visual delta:** Enormous ancient boar with one broken outer tusk, root-tangled mane, layered shoulder scars, and an extremely low unstoppable silhouette.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Create three successive stampede lanes before personally collapsing the remaining safe route.

### Teaching goal

Test straight lanes, collision terrain, curved furrows, and rotated barriers in one readable stampede puzzle.

### Signature mechanic

Three-Lane Stampede: each tusk scores a lane and assigns Bristle Hogs, Tunnel Boars, or Razorbacks to lead it before Old Tusk takes the final gap.

### Telegraph

The mane rises from tail to skull and the assigned lane leader stamps once inside its scored route.

### Counterplay

Interrupt a lane leader to preserve its gap, then redirect Old Tusk into the disabled route's obstacle.

### Lower-form interaction

Each lower form leads one route type while the War Tusker reshapes cover; removing a leader permanently preserves that lane during the boss phase.
