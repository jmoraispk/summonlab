A natural limbless snake family centered on fixed strike range, constriction, venom lanes, buried reach, and living coil control. It remains distinct from sapient humanoid Nagas.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Serpents family page](families/serpents.md).

**Escalation axis:** A fixed one-length strike becomes anchored constriction, a two-lane venom specialist, buried alternating reach, and finally a sovereign whose body coils become arena terrain.

**This form's visual delta:** Massive sand-adapted viper with paired eye horns, heavy keeled scales, thick tail, and multiple old shed scars.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Attack through alternating horn-marked emergence points while its exposed tail controls the safe route.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to infer the true buried head position from the deliberately misleading tail direction.

### Signature mechanic

Horn Gates: paired eye horns surface at alternating points while the tail indicates the gap opposite the real opening.

### Telegraph

One horn breaks the surface before each head strike and the exposed tail points away from it.

### Counterplay

Follow the tail direction to locate the real opening and punish the emerging neck after evading.

### Lower-form interaction

Adders occupy abandoned horn points, Constrictors hold the center, and the Cobra paints lanes toward the false opening.
