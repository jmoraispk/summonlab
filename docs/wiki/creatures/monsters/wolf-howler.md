A natural lupine expedition family built around pack geometry, pursuit, and coordinated prey selection. It remains distinct from transformed humanoid Werebeasts.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Wolves family page](families/wolves.md).

**Escalation axis:** Opportunistic flanking becomes alternating pursuit, directional pack timing, marked-prey command, and finally an arena-scale hunt circle controlled by an apex Greatwolf.

**This form's visual delta:** Tall narrow-chested wolf with a large pale throat ruff, deep rib cage, long muzzle, and a visibly working throat cavity.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Change the pack's attack rhythm by advancing allies on one side while the opposite wing holds position.

### Teaching goal

Introduce a qualitative pack-command pivot in which throat direction, not proximity alone, determines the next attack wing.

### Signature mechanic

Directed Howl: one directional howl releases the indicated wing while every Wolf on the opposite side pauses and braces.

### Telegraph

Its pale throat ruff expands and its muzzle points directly toward the wing that will advance.

### Counterplay

Interrupt the inhale or cross through the temporarily stationary wing before the advancing side closes.

### Lower-form interaction

Grey Wolves gain longer flank probes and Dire Wolves immediately swap pursuit sides when their wing is called.
