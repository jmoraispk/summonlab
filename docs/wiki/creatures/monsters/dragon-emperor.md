## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Command a phased territorial boss arena through four hoard-metal horizon pylons, each transforming the arena into one learned ground, hop, circuit, or roost grammar before the Emperor enters the exposed center.

### Teaching goal

Test mastery of claimed centers, fixed landings, inner flight anchors, and hoardfall routes by asking players to break the pylon supporting each phase while managing the corresponding lower Dragons.

### Signature mechanic

Imperial Circuit: four hoard-metal pylons face the arena horizons beneath the Emperor's storm-front wings. The Claw pylon causes Whelps to establish moving claims; the Wing pylon links Fledgling landing marks; the Crown pylon closes a Drake circuit; and the Hoard pylon lets an Elder Wyrm lay a coin roost. The Emperor branches its antler crown toward one pylon, conducts that lower-form phase, then lands on the central hoard with one body-warm heat shimmer and grows the selected pylon's metal across the matching armor quadrant. Breaking a pylon during the landing permanently removes both command and armor growth. After two breaks, the Emperor alternates ground and sky sides of the arena through the surviving grammar instead of repeating the Elder Wyrm at larger scale.

### Telegraph

One antler branch hooks toward a horizon, the matching hoard metal rings through the hide, the vast wings bank like a storm front, and every lower Dragon turns its head, horn, or landing marker toward the selected pylon.

### Counterplay

Follow the selected lower-form tell, disrupt its claims or route, attack the pylon when the Emperor commits to the central landing, and use the opened armor quadrant as shelter from the alternating ground-sky pass; each destroyed pylon permanently simplifies the circuit.

### Lower-form interaction

Whelps, Fledglings, Drakes, and an Elder Wyrm each operate one pylon's learned territorial grammar; destroying that pylon removes the relevant command, route benefit, and armor quadrant for the rest of the fight.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `planned-provisional-anchor` means candidate generation is authorized by the exact verified Normal candidate/result chain while human review remains pending. It implies neither approval nor runtime promotion.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
