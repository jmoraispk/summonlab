## Visual constants

- Every form remains limbless with a continuous scaled body, snake skull, jaw hinge, belly scutes, forked tongue, and species-appropriate tail.
- Tier escalation uses body proportion, hood, fangs, horns, crown scales, muscle distribution, and coil behavior rather than arms or equipment.
- Strike range, head anchor, venom direction, emergence point, and loosened coil remain visually traceable.

## T1–T5 combat progression

| Tier / function | Canonical form | Primary archetype | Battlefield duty | Teaching goal | Signature mechanic | Telegraph | Counterplay | Lower-form interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 — Initiate | [Adder](../serpent-adder.md) | Range-Probe Initiate | Coil once and strike exactly one body length. | Teach players to estimate a fixed biological strike range from the visible coil. | One-Length Strike: the Adder's extension never exceeds the length stored in its single tight coil. | Its neck forms a tight S and the forked tongue stops flicking. | Step beyond the fixed reach and punish the fully extended neck. | Not applicable for this form. |
| T2 — Soldier | [Constrictor](../serpent-constrictor.md) | Space-Hold Soldier | Loop around one obstacle or target and tighten only while its head remains anchored. | Teach players to distinguish the dangerous body loop from the vulnerable head anchor. | Anchored Coil: a completed tail loop tightens while the jaws maintain one visible anchor point. | The tail completes a circle and the jaw clamps firmly onto its anchor. | Break the loop or stagger the anchored head to release all constriction pressure. | Adders threaten the predictable outside edge while the Constrictor holds the central loop. |
| T3 — Specialist | [Spitting Cobra](../serpent-spitting-cobra.md) | Venom-Lane Specialist | Paint two visible venom lanes and threaten a conventional strike through the remaining gap. | Introduce a ranged specialist that edits escape geometry while preserving a physical throat and hood tell. | Two-Fang Fan: each fang projects one narrow venom line, leaving a deliberate central or outer gap for the follow-up strike. | Hood spots align with the two lanes and venom gathers separately at both fangs. | Close behind the hood or bait both spits away from the escape route before crossing. | Adders guard the chosen gap while Constrictors anchor obstacles that narrow the available lanes. |
| T4 — Champion | [Horned Viper](../serpent-horned-viper.md) | Buried-Reach Champion | Attack through alternating horn-marked emergence points while its exposed tail controls the safe route. | Teach players to infer the true buried head position from the deliberately misleading tail direction. | Horn Gates: paired eye horns surface at alternating points while the tail indicates the gap opposite the real opening. | One horn breaks the surface before each head strike and the exposed tail points away from it. | Follow the tail direction to locate the real opening and punish the emerging neck after evading. | Adders occupy abandoned horn points, Constrictors hold the center, and the Cobra paints lanes toward the false opening. |
| T5 — Sovereign | [Crown Serpent](../serpent-crown-serpent.md) | Living-Coil Sovereign | Encircle the arena in layered body coils and assign lower Serpents to the gaps between them. | Test fixed reach, head-anchor disruption, venom lanes, and buried emergence while treating the boss body as readable terrain. | Crown Coil: one crown scale lifts for each tightening body ring; the lower Serpent guarding that gap defines how it closes. | Crown scales lift in the order of the next constriction and the corresponding lower Serpent becomes still. | Remove or displace the still guard, cross the loosened coil, and strike the lifted crown scale before tightening resumes. | Adder supplies the fixed strike gap, Constrictor the anchored ring, Cobra the venom lane, and Horned Viper the buried opening; losing one permanently loosens its coil. |
