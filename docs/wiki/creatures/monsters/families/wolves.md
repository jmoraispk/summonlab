## Visual constants

- Every form retains a four-legged lupine skeleton, long muzzle, visible paws, expressive ears, and a readable tail; no form becomes humanoid or wears manufactured armor.
- Rank is communicated through chest mass, coat mantle, scars, posture, and pack behavior rather than arbitrary glow or simple scale.
- Pack attack direction remains readable through gaze, ear position, tail line, paw placement, and the spacing of companion Wolves.

## T1–T5 combat progression

| Tier / function | Canonical form | Primary archetype | Battlefield duty | Teaching goal | Signature mechanic | Telegraph | Counterplay | Lower-form interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 — Initiate | [Gray Wolf](../wolf-gray-wolf.md) | Flank-Probe Initiate | Circle toward an exposed side and commit to one tripping bite when the target turns away. | Teach players to track facing and deny a simple flank before learning full pack coordination. | Cross-Step Trip: the Gray Wolf crosses its outside forepaw inward before lunging at the target's rear leg. | Its shoulders drop and the outside forepaw crosses inward while its gaze fixes on the exposed leg. | Face the Wolf or step across its approach, then punish the missed bite during the low recovery. | Not applicable for this form. |
| T2 — Soldier | [Dire Wolf](../wolf-dire-wolf.md) | Hamstring Pursuer | Alternate pursuit with a packmate and slow isolated prey with a committed rear-leg bite. | Teach players to break two-sided pursuit geometry instead of retreating in a straight line. | Alternating Hamstring: only the Wolf on the prey's rear quarter attacks while its partner widens for the next exchange. | Its head flattens beside the shoulder as the partner visibly widens formation. | Break their angle with terrain or stagger the committed biter before the partner completes the swap. | Gray Wolves probe the opposite flank and create the turn that lets the Dire Wolf claim the rear quarter. |
| T3 — Specialist | [Howler](../wolf-howler.md) | Pack-Tempo Specialist | Change the pack's attack rhythm by advancing allies on one side while the opposite wing holds position. | Introduce a qualitative pack-command pivot in which throat direction, not proximity alone, determines the next attack wing. | Directed Howl: one directional howl releases the indicated wing while every Wolf on the opposite side pauses and braces. | Its pale throat ruff expands and its muzzle points directly toward the wing that will advance. | Interrupt the inhale or cross through the temporarily stationary wing before the advancing side closes. | Gray Wolves gain longer flank probes and Dire Wolves immediately swap pursuit sides when their wing is called. |
| T4 — Champion | [Pack Alpha](../wolf-pack-alpha.md) | Prey-Marking Champion | Mark one target, intercept attacks aimed at lesser Wolves, and swap the active pursuit wing after every lunge. | Teach players to separate a protective commander from the pack rather than tunneling into its guarded target. | Marked Quarry: a visible scent trail binds the Alpha's intercept to one target until terrain or distance breaks the line. | Its tail locks level, nose paints a visible scent line, and one foreleg braces toward the marked target. | Cross strong terrain to break the scent line or bait the intercept away before attacking the lesser Wolves. | It intercepts for Gray Wolves, completes Dire Wolf pursuit swaps, and changes wings on the Howler's cue. |
| T5 — Sovereign | [Greatwolf](../wolf-greatwolf.md) | Hunt-Circle Sovereign | Tighten an arena-scale hunt circle and assign every lower Wolf form to a readable breach point. | Test facing, pursuit swaps, howl direction, and commander separation as one coordinated pack encounter. | Closing Hunt: four breach points contract in sequence while the Greatwolf assigns Gray Wolves to probe, Dire Wolves to pin, the Howler to change tempo, and the Alpha to close the weakest exit. | Hackles rise in sequence around the circle and the Greatwolf's broken upper canine points toward the next closing breach. | Defeat or displace the assigned lower form and escape through its opening before the Greatwolf commits to that breach. | Each lower form owns one hunt-circle function; removing it keeps that breach open and permanently weakens the corresponding boss phase. |
