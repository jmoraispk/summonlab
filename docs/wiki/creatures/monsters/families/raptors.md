## Visual constants

- Every form retains an avian body with two wings, two taloned legs, hooked beak, feathered tail, and species-appropriate head rather than humanoid arms or torso.
- Tier escalation uses wingspan, feather structure, facial disc, talon mass, scars, and flight behavior rather than worn equipment.
- Shadow shape, tail fan, primary-feather angle, talon extension, perch choice, and landing route keep aerial attacks readable.

## T1–T5 combat progression

| Tier / function | Canonical form | Primary archetype | Battlefield duty | Teaching goal | Signature mechanic | Telegraph | Counterplay | Lower-form interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 — Initiate | [Carrion Kite](../raptor-carrion-kite.md) | Shadow-Dive Initiate | Circle once and dive when its shadow crosses the target. | Teach players to track an aerial threat through the ground shadow and punish the low recovery climb. | Shadow Crossing: the Kite commits exactly when its tightening shadow touches the target's feet. | The shadow tightens and the wings briefly stop beating. | Step across the shadow and punish the Kite during its low recovery climb. | Not applicable for this form. |
| T2 — Soldier | [Hunting Hawk](../raptor-hunting-hawk.md) | Repeated-Dive Soldier | Alternate two shallow dives from opposite directions and perch after the second. | Teach players to update facing between attacks and recognize a guaranteed grounded recovery. | Paired Passes: the Hawk's tail points toward the next opposite-side pass before both attacks, followed by a fixed perch. | Its tail fans toward the approach and the primary feathers whistle before each pass. | Change facing between dives and attack the clearly selected perch after the second. | Kites cross the ground shadow while the Hawk demands alternating vertical facing. |
| T3 — Specialist | [Night Owl](../raptor-night-owl.md) | Silent-Drop Specialist | Track movement rather than position and drop onto the last target that sprinted. | Introduce a qualitative specialist that changes safe movement cadence instead of merely adding another dive. | Last Motion Drop: the Owl fixes on the most recent sprint and descends silently onto that endpoint. | Its facial disc narrows and airborne dust stops moving beneath its approach. | Walk or hold still until the drop commits, then punish the open talons at ground contact. | Kite shadows encourage premature movement and Hawk passes alternate facing before the Owl selects the last sprint. |
| T4 — Champion | [War Eagle](../raptor-war-eagle.md) | Carry-Off Champion | Grip and lift one target a short distance while lesser Raptors attack the exposed group below. | Teach parties to interrupt a visible lift quickly or use its predictable landing route for a coordinated punish. | Committed Carry: lowered talons grip once, follow one shadow-marked ascent lane, and return through one fixed landing route. | Its legs lower, primary feathers spread, and the ascent lane lengthens in its shadow. | Break the grip before lift or attack the predictable landing route when the Eagle returns. | Lower Raptors pressure the group during the lift but must clear the Eagle's fixed return lane. |
| T5 — Sovereign | [Roc](../raptor-roc.md) | Airspace Sovereign | Create alternating high, crosswind, and ground-skimming flight bands before a full-span arena dive. | Test ground-shadow reading, alternating facing, movement restraint, and landing-route interruption across vertical space. | Three Flight Bands: successive feather rows lift to assign a lower Raptor to one altitude band before the Roc seals the remaining band with its own dive. | One feather row rises and its assigned bird calls once while the matching air lane gathers debris. | Clear the assigned bird to create a safe vertical pocket before the Roc commits to the other bands. | Kite controls the shadow band, Hawk the crossing band, Owl the still-air band, and War Eagle the landing band; defeating one permanently opens its altitude pocket. |
