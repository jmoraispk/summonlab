## Visual constants

- Every form retains a four-legged feline body, flexible spine, retractile claws, whiskered muzzle, mobile ears, and expressive tail.
- Species variation remains feline and is communicated by coat pattern, limb proportion, mane, canine length, and hunting posture rather than equipment.
- Eye line, tail stillness, shoulder compression, cover disturbance, and landing posture preserve readable attack direction.

| T1 — Initiate | [Wildcat](../great-cat-wildcat.md) | Brush-Pounce Initiate | Break line of sight once and pounce from the nearest low cover. | Teach players to watch cover disturbance and challenge a predictable first ambush. | Nearest-Cover Pounce: the Wildcat always uses the closest valid low cover after breaking sight. | Its tail tip stops and the chosen cover edge parts immediately before launch. | Face the opening or step toward it to force an early short pounce, then punish the landing. | Not applicable for this form. |
| T2 — Soldier | [Lynx](../great-cat-lynx.md) | High-Ground Soldier | Bound between raised perches and attack whichever target presents the longest exposed back. | Teach players to rotate facing vertically and contest predictable landing cones. | Perch Exchange: the Lynx uses two visible perches and must complete one grounded pause after each attack. | Ear tufts flatten and the selected perch sheds dust toward its landing cone. | Rotate toward the perch or vacate the cone, then punish the grounded pause. | Wildcats pressure low cover while the Lynx controls the high angle, but both share punishable landings. |
| T3 — Specialist | [Panther](../great-cat-panther.md) | Sightline Specialist | Move through connected dark cover and isolate one target by threatening allies who cross its marked line. | Introduce a positioning specialist whose still eye line controls movement without magical disappearance. | Unbroken Gaze: the Panther marks a line between its visible eyes and prey, then strikes the first other target to cross it. | Shoulder rosettes vanish sequentially into cover while the reflective eyes remain fixed and visible. | Flood the line with a deliberate feint or attack the still eye position before moving the isolated target. | Wildcats occupy nearby low cover and Lynxes threaten the elevated route around the marked line. |
| T4 — Champion | [Sabercat](../great-cat-sabercat.md) | Pinning Champion | Commit both long canines to a ground pin that gives lesser Cats safer approach angles. | Teach players to exploit a champion's strongest weapon as a temporary anchor and weak point. | Twin-Canine Pin: both sabers plant beside the target and cannot withdraw until the Sabercat resets its forequarters. | Its jaws open fully, forepaws widen, and heavy shoulders rise before the leap. | Dodge inside the leap and punish the planted canines or exposed ribs before withdrawal. | Wildcats and Lynxes use the pin's open sides while the Panther holds the direct rescue line. |
