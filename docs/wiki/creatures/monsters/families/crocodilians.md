## Visual constants

- Every form retains a low four-legged crocodilian body, long tail, scuted back, belly fault, sensory pits, and a jaw-driven attack silhouette.
- Tier escalation uses snout geometry, scute height, neck mobility, tail mass, scars, and waterline behavior rather than equipment or wings.
- Eyes, wake direction, jaw angle, foreclaw grooves, tail curve, and exposed belly keep ambush and rotation counterplay readable.

| T1 — Initiate | [Mire Caiman](../crocodilian-mire-caiman.md) | Waterline Initiate | Submerge along one visible ripple and snap once at the bank. | Teach players to track an aquatic ambush by its wake instead of staring at the last visible body position. | V-Wake Snap: the Caiman's eyes sink but its wake points continuously toward the fixed bank endpoint. | The eyes submerge while a narrow V-shaped wake remains visible. | Move parallel to the bank and punish the exposed tail after the missed snap. | Not applicable for this form. |
| T2 — Soldier | [River Crocodile](../crocodilian-river-crocodile.md) | Clamp Soldier | Lunge a short distance, clamp one target, and drag backward without turning. | Teach players to escape the jaw line or attack the planted towing limbs instead of the armored back. | Parallel-Groove Drag: the Crocodile's foreclaws cut two fixed grooves that define its backward towing path. | The jaw opens low and both foreclaws dig parallel grooves into the bank. | Sidestep the jaws or strike the planted forelegs during the backward drag. | Mire Caimans threaten adjacent bank points while the Crocodile commits to its straight drag. |
| T3 — Specialist | [Snarejaw](../crocodilian-snarejaw.md) | Bank-Drag Specialist | Sweep an elongated jaw around cover, close behind a target, and drag laterally along the shoreline. | Introduce a qualitative lateral grab that rewards moving toward the hinge rather than away from the teeth. | Crescent Snare: the far jaw tip traces a semicircle around cover before the hinge closes and drags sideways. | The far jaw tip taps the ground and the flexible neck bends into a visible crescent. | Step toward the head hinge or strike the narrow jaw side before closure. | Caimans occupy nearby water exits while River Crocodiles threaten the straight retreat from the crescent. |
| T4 — Champion | [Bastion Alligator](../crocodilian-bastion-alligator.md) | Armored-Rotation Champion | Present a scute wall and commit to a fixed-direction death roll that exposes its softer belly at regular intervals. | Teach players to read rotational direction and wait for a repeating armor gap rather than attacking the back. | Three-Beat Roll: locked side scutes protect two beats and expose the pale belly fault on the second rotation. | Side scutes lock together and the tail curves strongly against the coming rotation. | Evade away from the tail curve and strike the belly fault during the second roll. | Lower Crocodilians drive targets toward the roll lane but must also avoid its predictable rotating body. |
| T5 — Sovereign | [Primeval Crocodile](../crocodilian-primeval-crocodile.md) | Floodplain Sovereign | Open and close three water channels, route lesser Crocodilians into bank attacks, then cross the arena with a colossal roll. | Test wake tracking, clamp anchors, crescent grabs, and roll direction while the arena alternates wet and dry routes. | Three Flood Channels: the Primeval strikes channel gates in order, raising one hunting route and drying another before its crossing roll. | Its tail strikes the next channel gate and the corresponding waterline rises before any lower form enters. | Block the lower Crocodilian assigned to that channel, then use the resulting dry bank against the crossing roll. | Caimans reveal active wakes, River Crocodiles drag along straight banks, Snarejaw controls bends, and the Bastion Alligator occupies the final roll channel; disabling one preserves its dry route. |
