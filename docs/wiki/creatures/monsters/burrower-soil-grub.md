One segmented subterranean animal lineage built around ground reading, eruption timing, towing, false ridges, and tunnel networks. It is not a miscellaneous bin for unrelated cave creatures.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Burrowers family page](families/burrowers.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single moving ridge becomes a long eruption lane, hookjaw towing specialist, decoy-ridge champion, and persistent tunnel-network sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Pale compact segmented grub with small digging mandibles, thin sensory whiskers, and soft rear plates.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Travel just below the surface as a short moving ridge and emerge for one bite.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to read subsurface travel from a simple fixed sequence of ground pulses.

### Signature mechanic

Three-Pulse Bite: three evenly spaced soil pulses lead to one small emergence at the endpoint.

### Telegraph

Three compact soil humps travel in a straight line while the third widens around the head.

### Counterplay

Step perpendicular to the pulses and strike the soft body while it turns to re-enter the ground.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
