## Family visual grammar

- Squat crouched construction, hunched shoulders, horned beast face, clawed grip, and folded architectural wings remain recognizable.
- Sandstone, granite, marble, bronze, mortar, moss, and water staining remain discrete built materials rather than flesh.
- Relief carving, water-spout mouths, restrained amber seams, and pedestal fragments preserve the architectural source grammar.
- Stone quality and wing architecture escalate from useless carved flaps to sovereign buttresses while load-bearing joints stay readable.

**Escalation axis:** Material refinement and wing architecture progress from a perched chipped ornament to a sovereign architecture that commands its own statuary court.

**This form's visual delta:** Marble and verdigris bronze construction, spired horns, ornate carved armor, and wings framed like window tracery.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Control alternating aisles by opening marble-and-bronze wing tracery as a load-bearing screen.

### Teaching goal

Teach that bronze braces darken before each tracery aisle closes and can be broken from the open side.

### Signature mechanic

Rose-Window Guard: one ornate wing frames a closing aisle while the opposite tracery panel vents through a water-spout mouth.

### Telegraph

Spired horns align with an aisle and verdigris bronze drains from the brace about to carry the marble wing.

### Counterplay

Take the open aisle and break the darkening bronze brace before the framed wing closes.

### Lower-form interaction

Guardians dive along the framed aisle; breaking its brace forces them to land and exposes their hinges.
