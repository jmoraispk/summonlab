Source-draft Void Horrors are treated as a progressively unresolved absence-and-fracture visual and encounter-production family. Claims about extradimensional origin, ontology, intent, cognition, agency, personhood, and hostility remain proposals pending taxonomy review.

## Family visual grammar

- Rough humanoid or many-limbed absence retains edges that fail to close while proportions shift only within readable tier silhouettes.
- Matte void black, negative space, purple-black, star flecks, and one bounded cold eye accent remain source grammar rather than affinity evidence.
- Absence, non-reflection, star-field interior, fractured geometry, eye rings, and geometric shards never resolve into ordinary flesh or matter.
- Power subtracts coherence: later forms occupy less resolvable physical presence while telegraph anchors become deliberately sparse.

**Escalation axis:** Coherence is progressively subtracted from a thin-limbed crawler until the sovereign is an unresolved world-shaped absence with one readable eye-ring system.

**This form's visual delta:** Low scuttling Crawler of too many thin matte-black limbs with no face and no reflection.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Probe timing with one scuttling route whose final thin limb appears to skip a contact point.

### Teaching goal

Teach that the last matte limb pauses at an open edge even though no reflection confirms its position.

### Signature mechanic

Unfinished Step: too many thin limbs scuttle in sequence, but one missing contact makes the body hesitate before the low rake.

### Telegraph

Three limb tips align while a fourth open edge stops moving against the floor without reflection.

### Counterplay

Cross the missing-contact side and strike the paused thin-limb edge before the rake resumes.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.

### Status semantics

- `planned` means the active attempt is owned and may be produced; it does not mean an image exists.
- `blocked-on-anchor` means the active attempt must wait for accepted Normal anchor evidence before production.
- `candidate-only` means immutable candidate and result evidence exists; review approval has not occurred, and the image is not runtime art.
- `approved-review` means the immutable review evidence accepted the candidate; it is not runtime art.
- `revision-required` means review evidence requires another attempt. Where the active version has not been generated, the label reports the prior failed-attempt count.
- Candidate and result paths reserve collision-safe ownership. They are intentionally plain code paths, not links to nonexistent pixels.
- Runtime promotion is a separate integration decision and is never performed by this wiki export.
