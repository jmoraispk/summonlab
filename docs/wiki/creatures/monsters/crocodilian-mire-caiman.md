A natural quadrupedal reptile family centered on shoreline risk, armored facing, clamping, bank drags, death rolls, and floodplain control. It remains distinct from humanoid Lizardfolk and winged Dragons.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Crocodilians family page](families/crocodilians.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single waterline snap becomes a backward clamp, lateral bank-drag specialist, armored death-roll champion, and floodplain sovereign controlling multiple channels.

**This form's visual delta:** Small low caiman with mottled hide, narrow juvenile scutes, compact jaw, and thin tail.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Submerge along one visible ripple and snap once at the bank.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to track an aquatic ambush by its wake instead of staring at the last visible body position.

### Signature mechanic

V-Wake Snap: the Caiman's eyes sink but its wake points continuously toward the fixed bank endpoint.

### Telegraph

The eyes submerge while a narrow V-shaped wake remains visible.

### Counterplay

Move parallel to the bank and punish the exposed tail after the missed snap.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
