A natural bird-of-prey family centered on vertical tracking, attack shadows, landing windows, carry threats, and airspace control. It remains distinct from humanoid Harpies.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Raptors family page](families/raptors.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single shadow dive becomes alternating perch attacks, silent movement-triggered drops, carry-off pressure, and finally a sovereign that divides the arena into flight bands.

**This form's visual delta:** Lean long-winged kite with forked tail, small hooked beak, narrow chest, and ordinary mottled plumage.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Circle once and dive when its shadow crosses the target.

### Teaching goal

Teach players to track an aerial threat through the ground shadow and punish the low recovery climb.

### Signature mechanic

Shadow Crossing: the Kite commits exactly when its tightening shadow touches the target's feet.

### Telegraph

The shadow tightens and the wings briefly stop beating.

### Counterplay

Step across the shadow and punish the Kite during its low recovery climb.

### Lower-form interaction

Not applicable for this form.
