One segmented subterranean animal lineage built around ground reading, eruption timing, towing, false ridges, and tunnel networks. It is not a miscellaneous bin for unrelated cave creatures.

## Family context

Shared visual constants and the complete T1–T5 comparison live on the [Burrowers family page](families/burrowers.md).

**Escalation axis:** A single moving ridge becomes a long eruption lane, hookjaw towing specialist, decoy-ridge champion, and persistent tunnel-network sovereign.

**This form's visual delta:** Vast ringed Burrower with a petaled feeding maw, layered front armor, many tactile whiskers, and a vulnerable pale collar behind the head.

## Combat reading

### Battlefield duty

Open persistent tunnel mouths, route lower Burrowers through them, and collapse one arena sector each phase.

### Teaching goal

Test ridge reading, eruption endpoints, hook convergence, and true-route identification inside a controllable tunnel network.

### Signature mechanic

Feeding Network: tunnel lips contract in sequence toward a selected collapse while Deep Maw exposes its feeding collar to command traffic.

### Telegraph

Tunnel mouths contract in an ordered wave and the pale collar plates flare behind the petaled maw.

### Counterplay

Block a feeder tunnel, evacuate the contracting sector, then strike the exposed collar before the network reroutes.

### Lower-form interaction

Soil Grubs scout open mouths, Burrowing Worms erupt at endpoints, Hookjaws drag prey toward active routes, and Tremor Worms conceal the real collapse lane.
