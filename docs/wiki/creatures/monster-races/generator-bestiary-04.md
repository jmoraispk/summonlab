# Generator bestiary seed — races 19–24

## 19. Slimes

**What they are.** Amorphous blobs of translucent, corrosive jelly with no bones, face or fixed shape. A slime moves by flowing, eats by engulfing, and digests whatever it engulfs — so anything it has swallowed and not yet dissolved is visible suspended inside its body. Somewhere in the mass is a core, usually a denser glowing nucleus, which is both its brain and its weak point. As slimes grow they gain internal structure: first a defined core, then a crust, then something disturbingly close to a body held together by nothing.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Rounded, drooping, gravity-obeying blob; upper tiers approximate a humanoid outline without ever hardening into one.
- **Palette:** Translucent blue, acid green, amber, or violet — pick one per individual — with a brighter core color.
- **Material:** Viscous jelly, surface tension sheen, suspended debris, crystalline core.
- **Ornamentation:** Whatever is trapped inside — coins, bones, armor, weapons — plus surface crust and drip trails.
- **Escalation axis:** Internal structure emerging out of formlessness.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Droplet | Fist-sized, almost clear, a single dark dot for a core, one wobbling pseudopod. |
| T2 | Blob | Knee-high, cloudy, swallowed coins and small bones drifting inside, a slow drip trail behind it. |
| T3 | Ooze Knight | Roughly humanoid shape, a glowing crystalline core in the chest, a hardened crust across the shoulders like armor. |
| T4 | Devourer | Towering, with a full skeleton suspended and posed inside the mass, absorbed armor plates floating in place. |
| T5 | Primordial Ooze | A near-liquid titan with a dozen cores, absorbed weapons and armor arranged into the suggestion of a face, a tide of lesser slimes at its base. |

## 20. Golems

**What they are.** Constructed servants — bodies built out of inanimate material and animated by magic, with no life of their own and no free will. A golem has no face in the usual sense, only a carved mask or a blank front, and the magic that runs it shows as glowing runes or seams of light along the joints. They are made, not born, so quality of workmanship *is* rank: a village conjurer makes a lumpy clay figure, while a master artificer produces something of marble, gold and gemstone that looks more like a monument than a machine.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Blocky, heavy, thick-limbed humanoid with oversized hands, small or absent head, visible joint gaps.
- **Palette:** Clay red-brown, grey stone, granite, black basalt, white marble and gold at the top tiers. Rune-glow in blue, gold or orange.
- **Material:** Clay, fieldstone, iron banding, carved granite, basalt, marble, gold inlay, gemstone.
- **Ornamentation:** Carved runes, glowing seams, iron bindings, sculptural relief, a gemstone heart.
- **Escalation axis:** Material quality — clay, to stone, to marble and gemstone.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Clay Figure | Crude and lumpy, uneven limbs, fingerprints still in the surface, a single rune scratched on the forehead. |
| T2 | Stone Servant | Fieldstone body held together by iron bands, faint glowing seams at the joints, blank slab face. |
| T3 | Rune Warrior | Carved granite, properly proportioned, engraved runes covering the whole torso, stone fists like anvils. |
| T4 | Basalt Colossus | Huge black basalt body, plates floating slightly apart and held by magic, molten core visible through the chest cavity. |
| T5 | Master Golem | White marble with gold inlay, sculptural relief carved across every surface, a gemstone heart, a rune-halo hovering above the shoulders. |

## 21. Flame Elementals

**What they are.** Living fire given a body. An elemental has no flesh at all — it is combustion holding a shape, usually roughly humanoid, with limbs that trail into flame and no fixed face. Its temperature is legible in its color, exactly as real fire is: dull orange at the bottom, yellow-white in the middle, and blue-white at the hottest. The strongest have condensed a solid core at the center — a star, or a shell of cooled obsidian cracked open by the fire inside.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Roughly humanoid, wider at the top, limbs and head dissolving upward into flame. No feet — it tapers or hovers.
- **Palette:** Orange and yellow at low tiers, white-hot with blue edges at mid, blue-white with black obsidian at the top.
- **Material:** Open flame, ember, smoke, cooled obsidian crust, molten rock.
- **Ornamentation:** Ember showers, heat-shimmer distortion, floating cinders, cracked obsidian plating, flame crown.
- **Escalation axis:** Flame color temperature and how defined the core has become.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Wisp | Candle-sized flicker, dull orange, barely a shape, drifting cinders. |
| T2 | Flame Spirit | Human-sized humanoid fire, yellow-orange, no fixed face, arms trailing into smoke. |
| T3 | Blaze Warden | A white-hot core with blue edges, arms shaped into directed jets of flame, heat-shimmer aura. |
| T4 | Magma Lord | A cracked obsidian shell over living fire, molten rock running through the fissures, a mantle of smoke. |
| T5 | Conflagration Sovereign | A blue-white star at the center of the chest, solar corona around the whole body, a crown of standing flame, ground scorched in a ring. |

## 22. Treants

**What they are.** Trees that walk. A treant has a trunk for a body, roots for legs, branches for arms, and a face suggested in the bark — knots for eyes, a split for a mouth. They are slow, immensely strong, and immensely old; the eldest have been standing in one place for centuries and carry entire ecosystems on their bodies. Age is the only status they recognize, and it is written in girth, canopy size, and how much other life has taken up residence in them.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Vertical trunk-body, root-cluster legs, long branching arms, canopy spreading above the head.
- **Palette:** Bark brown and grey, deep moss green, autumn gold, and a warm amber glow at the heartwood.
- **Material:** Bark, heartwood, moss, lichen, sap, vine, stone caught in the roots.
- **Ornamentation:** Nesting birds, hanging vines, mushrooms, carved runes in the bark, growth rings visible at old wounds.
- **Escalation axis:** Age, girth, and how much other life is growing on the body.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Sapling-walker | Thin and whip-like, few leaves, shallow root feet, barely a face in the bark. |
| T2 | Young Treant | Thicker trunk, rough bark, moss across the shoulders, a proper canopy of leaves. |
| T3 | Oakheart | Broad oak-form, heavy root legs, dense foliage, vines hanging from the arms, clear bark face. |
| T4 | Elder Warden | Ancient and hollow-trunked, with an amber glow visible in the heartwood cavity, birds nesting in the branches, mushrooms along the base. |
| T5 | World-Tree Warden | A walking forest — a waterfall running down its back, boulders caught in the roots, a canopy that fills the sky, entire groves growing on its shoulders. |

## 23. Fungalfolk

**What they are.** Humanoid mushrooms. A fungalfolk has a stalk for a body, a cap for a head, gills underneath, and a network of mycelium threading through its pale, spongy flesh. They live in caves, rot and deep forest, communicate through spores rather than speech, and glow softly in the dark. They spread by releasing spores, so the more powerful ones are surrounded by a permanent visible cloud. Rank is cap size and luminosity — a sovereign's cap is a structure in its own right.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Cap-headed, stalk-bodied, slightly bulbous limbs, wider at the top than the base.
- **Palette:** Pale bone-white flesh, cap in one strong tone (crimson, violet, ochre or teal), bioluminescent cyan or green at the gills.
- **Material:** Fungal flesh, spongy cap tissue, mycelium threads, damp wood, moss, spore dust.
- **Ornamentation:** Spore clouds, glowing gill patterns, smaller mushrooms growing off the body, mycelium veins under translucent skin.
- **Escalation axis:** Cap size, bioluminescence, and spore density in the surrounding air.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Sporeling | Knee-high, plain brown cap, stubby limbs, no glow, faint puff of dust when it moves. |
| T2 | Mycon | Human-sized, spotted cap, faint cyan glow at the gills, a club of hardened stalk. |
| T3 | Sporeguard | Wide luminous cap, mycelium veins visible under translucent skin, a constant thin spore cloud around the shoulders. |
| T4 | Fungal Shaman | Cap spread like a parasol, glowing script written in suspended spores in the air, smaller mushrooms growing along the arms. |
| T5 | Mycelial Sovereign | A cap the width of a courtyard, a drifting spore aurora overhead, a throne of interlocked mushrooms, glowing mycelium spreading across the ground. |

## 24. Deep Ones

**What they are.** Fish-people from the ocean floor. They have humanoid bodies with fish or amphibian features — bulging lidless eyes, gill slits at the neck, webbed and clawed hands, scaled or slick eel-like skin, and a wide lipless mouth of needle teeth. They worship things in the deep and come ashore in raiding parties. The deeper and older a deep one is, the less human it looks: pressure-adapted bulk, tentacular growths, and anglerfish-style bioluminescent lures growing from the head.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Humanoid but hunched and long-armed, finned forearms and spine, wide flat head, hunched gill-neck.
- **Palette:** Drowned green, deep-sea blue-black, sickly grey, pearlescent belly white, with cold cyan or green bioluminescence.
- **Material:** Wet scale, eel skin, coral, barnacle, driftwood, rusted metal, whale bone.
- **Ornamentation:** Coral shard armor, barnacle crusting, fishing net, pearl and shell jewelry, glowing lures.
- **Escalation axis:** Loss of the humanoid look, and how much of the body produces light.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Fishman | Hunched and gasping, dull grey scales, visible gills, a rusted hook or gutting knife. |
| T2 | Tidewarrior | Finned forearms and calves, coral-shard armor over the chest, webbed claws, a barbed spear. |
| T3 | Abyssal Hunter | Broad and eel-skinned, a small glowing lure on the brow, a trident, barnacles crusting the shoulders. |
| T4 | Deep Priest | Tentacular beard and growths from the jaw, pressure-scarred plating, glowing organs visible through translucent skin, a staff of whale bone. |
| T5 | Leviathan Lord | Kraken-limbed and whale-scaled, colossal, a crown of anglerfish lures, robes of net and kelp, a court of lesser deep ones below. |
