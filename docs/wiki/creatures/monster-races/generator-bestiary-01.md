# Generator bestiary seed — races 1–6

Wiki ID: `monster-seed.generator-bestiary-01`

Design status: **source draft**. The entries below preserve the supplied visual bestiary wording for
generator and review use. “Race,” hostility, culture, cognition, rank, and personhood statements are
proposals, not accepted taxonomy or universal facts. Apply the [creature taxonomy](../taxonomy.md)
before promoting an entry to canon.

## 1. Goblins

**What they are.** Small, wiry humanoids roughly the size of a child, with green or grey skin, oversized pointed ears, hooked noses and mouths crowded with crooked teeth. They live in filthy warrens, caves and sewers in large numbers, and are individually weak but overwhelming in a mob. Goblins build nothing of their own — every tool, weapon and scrap of armor they carry was stolen, scavenged from a battlefield, or hammered together from broken pieces of somebody else's property. Their culture is a pecking order enforced by violence, and the biggest, luckiest and most heavily looted goblin rules the rest.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Short, hunched, long-armed, oversized head and ears, disproportionately large hands and feet.
- **Palette:** Sickly green and grey-green skin, muddy browns, rust-orange, dull iron. Accents of stolen gold.
- **Material:** Rags, patched leather, cracked wood, rusted scrap metal, bone, rope.
- **Ornamentation:** Trophies and loot — mismatched helmets, coin necklaces, teeth, stolen banners.
- **Escalation axis:** Scrap becomes treasure, and the posture straightens from a cringing crouch to an upright swagger.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Runt | Knock-kneed and cringing, filthy loincloth, bare feet, sharpened stick. Almost no gear. |
| T2 | Cutter | Stolen helmet three sizes too big, mismatched leather scraps, notched cleaver, small coin pouch. |
| T3 | Raider | Standing nearly upright. Riveted plate scavenged from three different armies, a real sword, a shield made from a door. |
| T4 | Warboss | Hulking for a goblin, gold-toothed, wearing a chestplate looted from a knight, dragging a banner of enemy helms. |
| T5 | Goblin King | Enthroned on welded loot, crown of fused crowns, cloak stitched from captured flags, rings on every finger, dais of coins. |

## 2. Orcs

**What they are.** Tall, heavily muscled humanoids with grey, green or ashen skin, jutting lower jaws and prominent tusks that grow up from the bottom lip. They are a warrior species organized into clans, where status is earned in battle and displayed openly on the body — war paint, scars, piercings, and trophies taken from the fallen. Orcs are strong enough that they favor heavy, brutal weapons over finesse, and their armor tends to be thick, riveted iron rather than anything elegant. A clan is led by whoever can physically dominate the others.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Broad, top-heavy, thick-necked, slightly forward-leaning stance. Wide shoulders, heavy jaw.
- **Palette:** Grey-green and ash-grey skin, blood red, black, dark iron, bone white. War paint in red and white.
- **Material:** Boiled leather, fur, riveted iron, bone, crude steel.
- **Ornamentation:** War paint, ritual scarring, tusk rings, skull trophies, spiked pauldrons, clan totems.
- **Escalation axis:** Muscle mass and tusk length — tusks grow longer and curve further up the face with each tier.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Grunt | Lean and sinewy, hide wraps, small blunt tusks, crude spear or hand axe. |
| T2 | Warrior | Broad-shouldered, red war paint across the eyes, iron-banded leather, heavy axe, first scars. |
| T3 | Berserker | Slab-muscled, tusks curling past the cheekbones, spiked pauldrons, twin cleavers, scar-lattice across the chest. |
| T4 | Warchief | Towering and thick as a door, tusks capped in iron, plate armor over fur, greataxe in each hand, skull totem on the back. |
| T5 | Overlord | Near-giant. Tusks the length of daggers, armor forged from a great beast's ribcage, cloak of hides, banner-pole of enemy standards. |

## 3. Giants

**What they are.** Enormous humanoids, anywhere from twice to fifty times the height of a person, built along the same basic lines as humans but far heavier in bone and limb. They live in mountains, glaciers and deep valleys, and the oldest of them are so still and so long-lived that stone, moss and even trees begin to grow on their bodies. A giant does not need weapons in any refined sense — an uprooted tree or a chained boulder does the job. The largest are less like people and more like landscape that has decided to walk.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Human proportions scaled up, but with heavier brow, thicker limbs and a slight stoop under its own weight.
- **Palette:** Stone grey, granite blue-grey, earth brown, moss green, glacier white. Warm ember tones in the eyes.
- **Material:** Rough hide, raw stone, moss, lichen, pine, iron chain, rope.
- **Ornamentation:** Chains, standing-stone jewelry, carved runes on the skin, whole trees used as decoration.
- **Escalation axis:** Raw scale, plus how much geology has spread across the body.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Brute | Twice human height. Rough hide clothes, a tree-trunk club, bare weathered skin. |
| T2 | Hill Giant | Four times human height. Moss on the shoulders, first stone plates forming along the back, rope belt. |
| T3 | Stone Giant | House-sized. Boulder-scale ridges down the arms, cliff-face brow, a megalith swung on a chain. |
| T4 | Mountain Lord | Mountain-tall. Waterfalls running off the shoulders, pines growing in the crags of its back, iron rings the size of gates. |
| T5 | Primordial Titan | A walking range. An entire valley on its back, glaciers in its beard, eyes like furnace mouths, clouds at the waist. |

## 4. Trolls

**What they are.** Tall, gangly, thick-skinned humanoids notorious for regenerating almost any injury within moments. They are ugly by design — warty, lumpen, long-armed, with sagging features and teeth that don't fit the mouth. The regeneration is the whole point of their look: because a troll's body heals faster than it heals *correctly*, an old troll is a catalogue of everything that has ever been done to it. Limbs regrow in the wrong place, wounds seal into ridges of scar, and extra features sprout where they shouldn't. They live under bridges, in caves and in bogs, and they eat more or less anything.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Tall, stooped, disproportionately long arms reaching past the knees, small head on a thick neck, potbelly.
- **Palette:** Bog green, liver-brown, sallow grey, bruise purple. Wet, glistening highlights.
- **Material:** Rubbery hide, scar tissue, bark-hard callus, mud, rope, scavenged rags.
- **Ornamentation:** Almost none by choice — piercings that healed over, bones embedded in flesh, half-swallowed weapons still lodged in the body.
- **Escalation axis:** Regeneration made visible — wounds that never close, and the accumulated wrongness of everything healed badly.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Scrag | Gangly and warty, half-healing gashes always open somewhere on the body, mud-caked. |
| T2 | Bog Troll | Thicker limbs, visible tumorous knots of regrowth on the shoulders, a rusted blade still stuck in its side. |
| T3 | Mutant Troll | Asymmetric — an extra arm on one side, a second jaw healed into the neck, ribs grown outward like armor. |
| T4 | Scarlord | A bloated mass of overlapping regrowth, bark-hard scar plating across the chest, mismatched limbs of different sizes. |
| T5 | Elder Troll | A walking landscape of unhealed and re-healed flesh. Multiple heads at different stages of forming, weapons of a dozen armies embedded in the hide. |

## 5. Dragons

**What they are.** Enormous winged reptiles with scaled hides, long necks and tails, clawed limbs, and a horned or crested skull. They are apex predators and, in most tellings, highly intelligent — vain, territorial and possessive, hoarding treasure in mountain lairs. A dragon's power is written in its size and armament: the span of its wings, the sweep of its horns, the thickness of its scale. They are born wingless and earthbound and only grow into the sky, so a dragon's age and rank can be read directly off its silhouette.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Long neck, long tail, four limbs plus wings, wedge-shaped horned skull, deep chest.
- **Palette:** Pick one metallic or jewel tone per individual (crimson, obsidian, gold, verdigris, storm-blue), with a paler underbelly and glowing eyes.
- **Material:** Overlapping scale, horn, bone spur, leathery wing membrane.
- **Ornamentation:** Horn crowns, spinal ridges, hoard-gold fused into the hide, battle scars, tattered membrane.
- **Escalation axis:** Wingspan and horn crown — both grow dramatically at each step.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Whelp | Wingless, wet-looking scales, oversized head on a small body, nubs where horns will come in. |
| T2 | Fledgling | Small membrane wings not yet strong enough to look reliable, one clean pair of horns, bright glossy scale. |
| T3 | Drake | Full-grown. Broad wings, a proper crown of horns, metallic gleam to every scale, spinal ridge running to the tail tip. |
| T4 | Elder Wyrm | Wings that blot out the sun, membrane torn and scarred, horns thickened and chipped, gold coins fused into the belly scales. |
| T5 | Dragon Emperor | An antler-like crown of branching horns, wings like storm fronts, heat-shimmer aura, hoard-metal grown into the armor of its hide. |

## 6. Skeletons

**What they are.** Human (or humanoid) skeletons animated by magic, walking and fighting with no flesh, organs or eyes. The magic that moves them usually shows as light burning in the empty eye sockets. Individually they are the flimsiest undead — a strong hit scatters them — but they are cheap to raise and never tire, so they are fielded in enormous numbers. The stronger the necromantic power invested in one, the denser and more fused its bones become, until the top of the ladder is barely a corpse at all but a sorcerer-king wearing its own skeleton as regalia.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Human skeleton proportions — narrow, angular, gaps of empty space where flesh should be.
- **Palette:** Bone white and yellowed ivory, rust, black iron, and one hot accent color for the socket-fire (green, blue or gold).
- **Material:** Bone, rusted mail, corroded steel, tattered grave-cloth.
- **Ornamentation:** Grave goods — funerary jewelry, crowns, bone spurs, orbiting bone fragments, carved runes on the skull.
- **Escalation axis:** Bone density and how much magic burns in the sockets.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Rattler | Loose, gap-toothed frame that looks about to fall apart. Dull pinpoints in the sockets, rusted shortsword. |
| T2 | Bonesoldier | Bound together in rusted mail, shield strapped to the forearm, weak ember glow in the eyes. |
| T3 | Bone Knight | Thickened, reinforced bone. Plated ribcage, helm fused to the skull, bright flame in the sockets. |
| T4 | Bone Champion | Bones fused into solid armor-like mass, horned skull, bone spurs rising off the shoulders like a crown. |
| T5 | Lich King | Skeletal but robed in gold and deep velvet, jeweled crown, staff of bound souls, halo of slowly orbiting bone fragments. |
