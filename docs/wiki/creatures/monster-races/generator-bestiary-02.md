# Generator bestiary seed — races 7–12

Wiki ID: `monster-seed.generator-bestiary-02`

Design status: **source draft**. The entries below preserve the supplied visual bestiary wording for
generator and review use. “Race,” hostility, culture, cognition, rank, and personhood statements are
proposals, not accepted taxonomy or universal facts. Apply the [creature taxonomy](../taxonomy.md)
before promoting an entry to canon.

## 7. Rotwalkers

**What they are.** Reanimated corpses — the classic zombie. They shamble, they do not speak, and they exist to spread the rot that made them. The lowest are barely holding together, sagging and slow. What makes them frightening at higher tiers is not that they decay further, but that the plague animating them becomes *more* effective: the muscles cord up, the bone reinforces, and eventually several corpses are pulled together into one larger body. They are always wet, always partly open, and always trailing the marks of whatever killed them.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Human, but off — dislocated shoulders, dragging leg, head at a wrong angle, jaw hanging.
- **Palette:** Gray-green and bruise-purple flesh, dried blood brown, bile yellow, dirty linen white.
- **Material:** Rotting flesh, exposed sinew, wet bone, grave-dirt, torn burial clothes.
- **Ornamentation:** Whatever they died in — funeral clothes, work aprons, armor rusted onto the body, the weapon that killed them still embedded.
- **Escalation axis:** Decay reversing into cohesion — higher tiers are *more* physically integrated, not less.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Shambler | Sagging skin, sunken eyes, dragging one foot, grave dirt everywhere, clothing rotted to strips. |
| T2 | Ghoul | Fresher and faster. Exposed sinew along the arms, clenched jaw, hands hooked into claws. |
| T3 | Rot Brute | Muscle-corded and bloated, bone burst through the forearms into natural blades, ribcage cracked open. |
| T4 | Amalgam | Multiple corpses fused into a single hulking body — extra arms, two torsos, several heads folded into the mass. |
| T5 | Plague Lord | A cathedral of fused bodies with one central grinning face, robes of stitched shrouds, a haze of flies, throne of the dead. |

## 8. Wraiths

**What they are.** Incorporeal undead — the spirit of a dead person that stayed behind and turned hostile. A wraith has no solid body. It appears as a hooded, robed or smoke-like figure, often with nothing inside the hood but cold light where eyes should be, and its lower half typically trails away into vapor rather than ending in feet. They are cold to be near, pass through walls, and drain life by touch. The weakest are barely more than a smear of fog. The strongest have condensed into a defined, almost regal shape, closer to a monarch than a ghost.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Hooded, robed, upper body defined and lower body dissolving into trailing vapor. No visible feet.
- **Palette:** Black, charcoal, deep indigo, ash gray. One cold accent — pale blue, white or sickly green — for the eyes.
- **Material:** Smoke, tattered cloth, cold vapor, rusted chain, hollow armor.
- **Ornamentation:** Chains, funeral shrouds, spectral crowns, floating rings of frost.
- **Escalation axis:** From formless mist to a sharply defined regal silhouette.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Shade | A faint smear of vapor with two cold points of light. Barely holds a humanoid outline. |
| T2 | Haunt | A hooded shape with trailing tatters, thin skeletal suggestion of hands, dragging chain. |
| T3 | Wraith | Clearly defined robed figure, skeletal hands fully visible, spectral blade, chains orbiting the body. |
| T4 | Crowned Specter | A crowned figure whose cloak spreads outward into a black tide, frost forming on everything nearby. |
| T5 | Death Sovereign | Hollow ornate armor filled with void, scythe of frozen smoke, robe like a night sky, a court of lesser shades trailing behind. |

## 9. Vampires

**What they are.** Undead that survive by drinking blood, and who retain their intelligence, memory and appearance in death. Vampires occupy a strange double register: the newly turned are feral, filthy and rat-like, closer to an animal than a person, while the ancient ones are impossibly elegant — pale, still, beautifully dressed, and utterly inhuman behind the manners. Fangs, claws, red eyes and a total absence of color in the skin are constant. Rank in vampire society is age, and age shows as refinement.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Human and tall, unnaturally still, long-fingered. Higher tiers gain a cloak or wing-cloak that widens the shape.
- **Palette:** Corpse white and bone pallor, black, deep blood red, aged gold. No warm skin tones anywhere.
- **Material:** Velvet, silk, brocade, polished leather, gold, blood-glass, bat membrane.
- **Ornamentation:** Signet rings, high collars, jeweled clasps, medals, ornate cane, family crests.
- **Escalation axis:** Bestial to aristocratic — rags become couture as the posture becomes perfect.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Thrall | Feral and hunched, filthy, rat-like teeth, bloodstained shift, clawed fingers, animal posture. |
| T2 | Bloodkin | Pale hunter in a dark coat, long claws, hair slicked back, fangs prominent, still slightly hunched. |
| T3 | Noble | Fully upright and composed. Tailored velvet, red eyes, gloves, an ornate blade or cane. |
| T4 | Ancient | An unnervingly still lord in a bat-membrane cloak, ornate rings on every finger, a face that hasn't aged in centuries. |
| T5 | Progenitor | Inhumanly beautiful, wings of pure shadow, throne of blood-glass, a crown so fine it looks grown rather than made. |

## 10. Mummies

**What they are.** Preserved corpses wrapped in linen bandages and buried with ritual and treasure, risen to walk again. The lowest are simply dried-out husks in crude wrappings, dragging themselves out of a common grave. The higher ranks were important people in life, so their preservation was expensive: gold bands sewn into the linen, jeweled collars, protective glyphs inked along every wrapping, and finally a full death-mask of beaten gold. Sand and dust follow them. The bandages never come off — they simply become more valuable.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Human, stiff-limbed, arms often held forward or crossed over the chest. Wrapped from head to foot.
- **Palette:** Sun-bleached linen, sand beige, desert ocher, deep gold, lapis blue, turquoise.
- **Material:** Linen bandage, dried flesh, gold leaf, lapis, carnelian, sandstone.
- **Ornamentation:** Scarab charms, jeweled pectoral collars, glyph inscriptions, gold arm bands, headdresses.
- **Escalation axis:** The wrappings themselves become royal gold.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Husk | Crude gray bandages, one leg dragging, dried flesh showing through gaps, empty eye holes. |
| T2 | Tomb Guard | Taut and dry, bronze charms sewn into the linen, a bronze khopesh, simple headcloth. |
| T3 | Sacred Servant | Gilded bands wound over the wrappings, jeweled collar, glyphs glowing faintly along the bandages. |
| T4 | Pharaoh's Champion | Full gold death-mask, ceremonial armor over the linen, sand swirling constantly at the feet. |
| T5 | God-King | Towering headdress, gold and lapis regalia, a floating sarcophagus lid behind him like a banner, a permanent sandstorm halo. |

## 11. Demons

**What they are.** Malevolent spirits given physical form, native to a burning underworld and organized into a strict infernal hierarchy. The common visual grammar is horns, hooves or clawed digitigrade legs, red or ashen skin, bat-like wings, and fire burning somewhere inside the body. A demon's rank is legible at a glance: the more horns and the more of the body that has stopped being flesh and become live fire or obsidian, the higher it stands. The lowest are pests the size of a cat. The highest sit on thrones of magma and command legions.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Horned head, broad shoulders, digitigrade or hooved legs, long tail, bat-membrane wings.
- **Palette:** Blood red, char black, ember orange, molten yellow, obsidian. Glowing cracks throughout.
- **Material:** Charred hide, obsidian, blackened iron, molten rock, smoke, bat membrane.
- **Ornamentation:** Brands and infernal sigils burned into the skin, chains, spiked black armor, skull motifs.
- **Escalation axis:** Horn count and how much of the body is fire rather than flesh.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Imp | Cat-sized, red, pinprick horns, small bat wings, whip-thin tail, needle teeth. |
| T2 | Fiend | Human-sized, goat-legged, one pair of curling horns, ember cracks opening in the skin, brand marks. |
| T3 | Hellknight | Broad-chested, four horns, molten veins visible under the hide, black spiked plate, flaming blade. |
| T4 | Archfiend | Twice human height. Obsidian armor fused to the body, wings of black smoke, a crown of horns, chains of bound souls. |
| T5 | Demon Prince | Enthroned in magma. Horns branching into a corona above the head, body more fire than flesh, wings spanning the whole frame. |

## 12. Celestials

**What they are.** Winged beings of light and order — angels, in the broad sense. They appear as idealized humanoids in gleaming armor or white robes, with feathered wings and a ring or geometric halo above or behind the head. Their rank is expressed almost mathematically: the higher the being, the more pairs of wings it has and the more complex its halo becomes, until the face is covered entirely and the body itself is mostly light. They are beautiful but not warm — the highest ranks are closer to a mechanism than a person.

**Visual constants**
- **Silhouette:** Idealized upright humanoid, symmetrical, feathered wings, halo above the head.
- **Palette:** White, ivory, pale gold, sunlight yellow, sky blue. Radiant glow.
- **Material:** Polished gold, white marble, feather, silk, pure light.
- **Ornamentation:** Halos, ring geometry, sacred script floating in the air, ribbons, censers, blank ceremonial masks.
- **Escalation axis:** Number of wings and the geometric complexity of the halo.

| Tier | Name | Visual |
|------|------|--------|
| T1 | Messenger | Small and slight, two soft wings, plain robes, a simple ring of light, bare feet. |
| T2 | Guardian | Armored in gold plate, full feathered wings, a solid ring halo, spear and round shield. |
| T3 | Warden | Four wings. Ornate gold armor, a halo of two rotating rings, script drifting in the air around the arms. |
| T4 | Archon | Six wings, faceless masked helm, halo of interlocking geometric rings, sword of pure light. |
| T5 | Seraph | Wings beyond counting layered in tiers, body mostly light with only a suggestion of form, halo like a working orrery. |
